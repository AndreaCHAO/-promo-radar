Contest – JustMarkets Golden Challenge

1.2. The total prize pool for the Contest consists of:
20 gms of Gold or money prize 3000 USD x3;
10 gms of Gold or money prize 1500 USD x5;
5 gms of Gold or money prize 750 USD x7;
money prize 1000 USD x10;
money prize 500 USD x10;
money prize 300 USD x10;
money prize 200 USD x 40.
1.3. Contest Period: 2nd of November 2026 to 31st of January 2027 (all dates inclusive).
1.4. By participating in the Contest, each Participant fully agrees to comply with these Terms and Conditions.
2. How to enter the JustMarkets Great Golden Challenge
2.1. To participate in the Contest:
Be persons who can form legally binding contracts under the laws. Without limiting the foregoing, participation in the Contest is not allowed for persons under the age of 18 or otherwise under legal age in their country of residence (“minors”).
Be an active, registered and verified Client of the JustMarkets or register one or more new trading accounts.
To deposit at least 100 USD for an account or maintain an account balance of 100 USD. Only accounts deposited with the minimum required amount can be registered in the Contest. The internal transfer of money from one account to another account is not considered as a deposit.
To sign up for the Contest, go to the Personal Area, navigate to the ‘Benefits’ section, select the account you wish to participate with, and enter your nickname. Only Standard, Pro, and Raw Spread accounts can participate in the Contest. Demo accounts do not qualify Clients to enter into the Contest.
To trade:
3 universal lots or more during the current week (from Monday till Sunday) within the Contest Period on Standard or Pro or Raw Spread account that was registered in the Contest – to participate in Weekly Lucky Draw.
10 universal lots or more during the Contest Period on Standard or Pro or Raw Spread account that was registered in the Contest – to participate in the Final Lucky Draw.
* A universal lot is defined as 100,000 USD in trading volume (opened and closed). Different instruments have different contract sizes and prices, so the volume created by 1 universal lot is not always the same.
Please note that the value of instruments can vary significantly.
Please see example: To open and close deals for 1 lot (100 contracts) of XAUUSD, it is approximately 392,000 USD.
2.2. Only one trading account registered by the Client may participate in the Contest. The universal lots traded on several trading accounts isn’t summed up.

The leaderboard, which will be updated daily on JustMarkets website, will reflect the cumulative Points of each Client reflecting a certain nickname. 4. Selecting the winners of the Final Lucky Draw
4.1. After the Contest ends, winners will be randomly selected from Clients who have fulfilled the conditions in clause 2, using a digital randomizer tool – the Wheel of Fortune.
4.2.Participants will be divided into three tiers in accordance with the volume of trading during the Contest Period:
Tier 1 Сlients are those who traded 100 lots or more during the Contest Period;
Tier 2 Сlients are those who trade between 50 and 99 lots during the Contest Period;
Tier 3 Clients are those who trade between 10 and 49 lots during the Contest Period.
4.3. The winners in each Tiers will be determined through a random draw as follows:
Tier 1 Prize : money prize of 1000 USD – 10 Final Lucky Draw winners;
Tier 2 Prize : money prize of 500 USD – 10 Final Lucky Draw winners;
Tier 3 Prize : money prize of 300 USD – 10 Final Lucky Draw winners.
4.4. A Participant may win Final Lucky Draw only once. If a Participant is selected as a winner more than once during the random draw process, only their first selection will be considered valid, and any subsequent selections will be disregarded and replaced with another eligible Participant.
5. Conducting the Weekly Lucky Draw
5.1. In addition to the main Contest and Final Lucky Draw, a Weekly Lucky Draw will be held throughout the Contest Period.
5.2. Each week, all Clients who have fulfilled the conditions in clause 2, will automatically be entered into the Weekly Lucky Draw. Each eligible Client will have equal chances of winning the weekly prize, regardless of the number of lots traded. Each Client has 1 chance to win. 5.3. The winners will be determined through a random draw – Wheel of fortune, each Tuesday within all qualified Participants for the last week (from Monday till Sunday).
5.4. In each Weekly Lucky Draw, 5 winners will be selected. The Weekly Lucky Draw will take place over 12 weeks during the Contest Period, and each winner will be awarded a money prize of 200 USD.
5.5. A Participant may win only once per week. If a Participant is selected as a winner more than once during the random draw process, only their first selection will be considered valid, and any subsequent selections will be disregarded and replaced with another eligible Participant.
