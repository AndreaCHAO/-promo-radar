Explore and Join 3 Promos for IB Partners
Celebrate October with rewards! From October 1 to October 31, FBS invites IB partners from Africa, Latin America, and Thailand to take part in three exclusive promos. Grow your network, compete for prizes, and shine with the FBS Partner community.
Why Join the FBS IB Program?
The FBS IB Program is one of the most recognized in Asia, giving partners the opportunity to build their own business without investment. IBs earn commissions of up to 43% from every trade their clients make, credited daily to their Partner account.
Beyond commissions, FBS provides educational resources to help IBs expand their communities. Partners can host webinars and seminars in their region using FBS’s materials, while building their brand under a broker trusted by millions worldwide.
FBS also ensures excellent trading conditions for clients, starting with deposits from just $5. Traders can choose from more than 100 withdrawal methods available 24/7, including local banks and global providers, and enjoy fast, secure automated withdrawals anytime.
With more than 700 000 partners globally and over 100 international awards, FBS has earned recognition for its strong IB program. Recent honors include:
