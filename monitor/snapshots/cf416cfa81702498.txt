FAQ
Common questions
Why is my points balance zero when I trade every day? + Almost always because the account is MT4 . TN Points needs to know how long a position stayed open, which it works out by matching the opening order to the closing one. MT5 records trades that way; MT4 records a trade as a single operation with no separate open and close, so the hold time cannot be measured and no points are awarded. This is a limitation of how the platform records trades, not a penalty — if loyalty matters to you, trade on MT5. The next two most common reasons are closing positions in under 5 minutes, and trading an instrument outside the majors and gold.
How do I start earning points? + Open a TNFX account, then activate TN Points from your client area. Points start counting from your activation.
Which trades earn points? + Real-money forex and gold (XAUUSD) trades held longer than 5 minutes. Points are added automatically.
What is a point worth? + 1 point = $1 when you take it as a trading bonus. As cash to your wallet, points convert at 12 to 1 (120 points = $10).
