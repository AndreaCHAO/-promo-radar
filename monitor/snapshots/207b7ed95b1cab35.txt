STARTRADER Expands STAR Copy with New Promoter Features in 2.0 Update
FinanceWire
November 10, 2026
Share
Preferred source
English 日本語 한국어 繁體中文 ไทย Português Italiano Deutsch Français Español
Port Louis, Mauritius, November 10th, 2026, FinanceWire
The update adds strategy promotion, cross-platform sharing, and guest browsing to STAR Copy.
STARTRADER has released STAR Copy 2.0, an update that brings strategy creation, discovery, promotion, and copying into one experience across Web, H5, and App. The release introduces new features for Signal Providers, Copiers and Promoters.
At the centre of the update is Strategy Promoter, which allows users to promote public strategies through dedicated referral links. Eligible Promoters may receive a share of the profit-sharing fees generated by eligible Copiers joining through their links, subject to applicable terms.
For Signal Providers seeking distribution, Elite Promoter introduces configurable promoter networks of up to 30 levels, with tools to define commission distribution and access reporting on promoter activity and fees, while Promoters gain clearer reporting on their own activity.
Shared links now direct users to the appropriate platform while preserving referral attribution, and Guest Mode lets visitors explore STAR Copy without logging in.
STARTRADER sees copy trading becoming more community-driven, with discovery and distribution as important as strategy creation, a shift it calls the Copy Economy. Guided by its client-first approach, the company is focused on giving clients clearer ways to explore strategies and make informed decisions about whether to take part.
“We see copy trading moving from individual decisions to connected communities. STAR Copy 2.0 gives the people who build and share strategies the tools to reach that community, within a clear and transparent structure.” — Peter Karsten, CEO, STARTRADER
STAR Copy 2.0 marks the next step in how STARTRADER approaches copy trading: building the tools that help strategies reach the right audience, while keeping clients’ choice, clarity and transparency at the centre.
About STARTRADER
STARTRADER is a global multi-asset broker empowering retail and institutional partners to access global markets through platforms including MetaTrader, STARTRADER APP and STAR Copy.
STARTRADER operates through entities licensed and regulated by authorities including CMA, ASIC, FSCA, FSA and FSC, combining strong governance with a client-first approach and a commitment to transparency, reliability and long-term growth.
Risk Warning
CFDs are complex instruments and carry a high risk of losing money rapidly due to leverage. Copy trading also involves risks, and copying another trader’s strategy does not guarantee similar results or profits. Ensure you understand the risks before trading.
