EBC Beyond Market Brings Academic-Industry Dialogue to Universitas Indonesia
Summary:
EBC Financial Group Indonesia and Universitas Indonesia held a seminar on data literacy, market analysis and financial education.
Amid the massive flow of financial information in the digital age, the ability to read economic data objectively has become a crucial skill for the younger generation. Responding to this challenge, EBC Financial Group Indonesia, in collaboration with the Management Student Society of the Faculty of Economics and Business at Universitas Indonesia (MSS FEB UI), successfully held an educational seminar on Thursday, 17 September 2026.
Held at the R. Soeria Atmadja Auditorium, FEB UI Campus, the event titled "EBC Beyond Market: Reading the Market, Understanding the Global Economy" was designed to equip students with analytical tactics to respond to market dynamics, ensuring they do not merely get caught up in trends or the Fear of Missing Out (FOMO).
Bridging Theory and Reality Through Academic-Industry Dialogue
More than just a standard public lecture, this event was driven by the Academic-Industry Dialogue concept. This collaborative forum serves as a strategic space that brings together global financial industry practitioners and the best minds from the academic community. This approach bridges the educational gap by aligning classroom macroeconomic theories with the fast-paced realities of financial markets.
The interactive discussion was built upon a data-driven point-of-questions framework. Through this structured yet adaptive approach, industry insights were transferred so that students can design critical, analytical, and implementable risk mitigation strategies.
Dissecting the Market from Two Perspectives
The dialogue session combined two distinct areas of expertise through the presence of Sana Ur Rehman, Financial Market Analyst at EBC Financial Group, and Ibrahim Kholilul Rohman, Senior Research Associate at IFG Progress and Lecturer at the Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Indonesia. Rehman, who brings nearly a decade of global experience in macroeconomics, foreign exchange, and commodities, emphasised the importance of data literacy.
"Macroeconomic literacy is no longer just an option for today's generation," Rehman asserted. "Central bank decisions, trade policy shifts, or geopolitical shocks now reach students' phone screens through social media feeds before they make it into textbooks. The real skill is learning to read the data itself, not just blindly swallowing market reactions."
These insights were enriched by Rohman, who has a 20-year track record in the digital economy, financial policy, and logistics, including his strategic experience at the European Commission. (Note: Corrected from "Sana" in the original text to reflect Rohman's actual background).
"Synergy between academia and industry is crucial in this dynamic economic era," Rohman stated. "Students are equipped with a strong theoretical foundation in the classroom, but they also need practical insights into how global policies and market sentiments interact in the real world. Through continuous dialogues like this, together we are shaping young talents who are not reactive to rumours, but responsive and critical of data."
Concrete Steps to Improve National Financial Literacy
Based on the 2024 OJK-BPS Survey, Indonesia's national financial literacy index stood at 65.43%. As the next generation that will dominate the investment landscape, university students are a strategic audience for literacy enhancement. To maximize participation, MSS FEB UI integrated this seminar into its credit-bearing programming. Participants from the 2022 cohort and earlier received three KUM points, while those from the 2023 cohort and later earned five KUM points, complemented by e-certificates and door prizes.
Global Commitment to Continuous Education
This initiative at Universitas Indonesia is part of EBC's broader global academic partnership program. The company has renewed its three-year agreement with the University of Oxford's Department of Economics for public economic education and holds an official foreign exchange partnership with FC Barcelona.
In Indonesia, EBC Financial Group's commitment extends beyond academic education. Commemorating Independence Day, EBC recently held a demo trading competition to promote risk literacy, and the company continues to run the "Berbagi Berkah" initiative, which consistently channels social support to children at Yayasan Pondok Kasih Mandiri.
