What is the Partner Loyalty Program?
Earn bonuses based on your accumulated partner commission.
How it works
Our loyalty program rewards active partners. It’s simple — just accumulate partner commission and get prizes. Briefly about the features and rules of the program:
The more partner commission you accumulate, the higher your tier.
The program resets once the final tier is reached
Available to all partners by default
Prizes may be substituted with a cash equivalent.
Eligible prizes are processed within 10 business days after confirmation.

FAQ
What happens after reaching Platinum tier?
The program resets. You begin again from Common Tier and can earn all rewards again.
What counts toward accumulated commission?
All partner commissions earned from your referred clients.
When is the reward issued?
Either automatically (up to 10 days) or upon request – depending on the reward type.
Can I skip a tier?
No, tiers must be completed in order.
Will my past commissions be counted toward the program?
No. Only commissions earned after the program launch date count toward your progress.
