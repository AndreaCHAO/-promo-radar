XS.com Brings the Partnership Community Together at Morocco Seminar
XS.com, the award-winning global multi-asset broker, brought its partner community together in Casablanca on September 4 for an exclusive seminar focused on “The Future of Partnership with XS,” reinforcing the global broker's continued commitment to engaging with partners and supporting their growth across key markets.
Held at the Hyatt Regency Casablanca, the seminar welcomed 100+ partners and industry professionals for a dedicated forum focused on business development, collaboration, and the evolving opportunities within the global trading industry.
The event brought together senior representatives from XS.com, who shared insights into the broker’s approach to partnerships, the changing dynamics of the trading industry, and the opportunities available within the broader XS.com ecosystem. Their participation provided attendees with direct access to the XS.com’s leadership perspective while creating an open environment for discussion and exchange.
With partner recruitment, education, and motivation at the heart of the program, the seminar explored the opportunities and challenges shaping the partner landscape. Discussions looked beyond current market conditions to consider how partners can adapt to an increasingly competitive financial-services environment, develop their businesses, and build stronger, more sustainable relationships.
A key highlight of the event was “The IB Growth Lab,” a dedicated session focused on Introducing Broker growth. The session explored practical approaches to developing an IB business, expanding professional networks, strengthening client acquisition strategies, and identifying new avenues for long-term growth. By placing the IB perspective at the center of the discussion, the session added a practical and business-focused dimension to the seminar.
The Casablanca event also provided XS.com with an opportunity to engage directly with partners in the Moroccan market and gain deeper insight into their priorities, challenges, and expectations. Discussions throughout the seminar extended beyond the formal agenda, allowing attendees to exchange experiences and explore potential areas for future collaboration.
The evening continued with an open buffet and networking session, giving attendees an informal setting to build new professional connections, strengthen existing relationships, and continue conversations from the day’s sessions.
The seminar reflects XS.com’s continued focus on developing its global partner ecosystem through direct engagement, education, and market-specific initiatives. By creating opportunities for partners to connect with senior representatives, exchange market perspectives, and gain practical business insights, XS.com is reinforcing its commitment to partnerships as an important driver of long-term growth.
XS.com Company Review
The XS Group (operating under brand name “XS” or “XS.com”) is a Global Multi-Asset Broker providing access to trade a wide range of financial products.
Established in Australia in 2010, XS.com has grown into a global market leader in the FinTech, financial services and online trading industry with licences in various jurisdictions and offices in different locations around the globe.
XS.com offers traders, institutional investors and brokers worldwide access to deep institutional liquidity and advanced trading technology, combined with an efficient user experience, high-quality relationship management and excellent customer support.
