RZR Funded Adds Prediction-Market Challenges after Hantec Trader Rebrand
2026-11-16T21:06:50.230+02:00
M o n d a y , 1 6 / 1 1 / 2 0 2 6 | 1 9 : 0 6 G M T by
Tanya Chepkova
RZR Funded now lets participants complete one-stage simulated prop challenges using prediction markets.
Prices follow external prediction markets, but no real contracts are traded.
Retail FX
prop trading
A peek at the firm's rebranded website.
RZR Funded (the former Hantec Trader) have launched one-stage prediction market challenges as an extension of their simulated trading model which previously covered currencies, indices and commodities.
The new product enables participants to take virtual positions on over 5,000 events across politics, sport, economics, crypto and weather. Those who pass the screening will be awarded a simulated funded account and may be eligible for cash rewards based on their virtual profits.
The launch is aimed at people who follow the elections, the football or the economic decisions but who may not have any real experience of the traditional financial markets.
It's also the first new product launched since Hantec Trader took on the RZR Funded brand in October.

Contracts Follow Third-Party Market Prices
Participants don't trade directly on a prediction market exchange. RZR Funded's terms state that all positions are simulated and priced against Polymarket or another third party selected by the company.
RZR Funded do not submit customer orders to the reference platform and the participant obtains no interest in an actual contract. Settlements are on the outcome of the relevant external market event, although the company may delay or vary the settlement should the event in question be in dispute, cancelled or otherwise altered.
This is different from opening an account with a prediction market broker or exchange where you pay for access to the evaluation and your maximum direct loss is the challenge fee rather than the balance that you see in your account.

Challenges Start at $18
One stage evaluation requires a 7% profit and total losses cannot exceed 4% of the starting virtual balance. There is no individual daily loss limit.
Account sizes are between $1k and $100k with fees starting at $18. RZR Funded has a reward share of up to 90% and reward requests every 7 days.
The programme is available via the company's existing dashboard.
Access is restricted in jurisdictions where prediction markets or gambling are prohibited.
Participants are also prohibited from trading on events where they have non-public information, where they can influence the outcome or where they work for the reference market.
New Product Follows October Rebrand
Hantec Trader launched in 2023 and rebranded to RZR Funded last month . They state that their prior link to a globally regulated broker had underpinned the business but had also restricted the range of products and markets it could go on to develop on its own.
At that point RZR Funded confirmed that its existing team, customer accounts, challenge rules and reward criteria would all stay exactly the same.
Its legal disclosures also make it very clear that RZR Funded and Hantec Markets Limited are completely separate entities.
The prediction market launch is therefore coming from the independent prop firm, not the regulated Hantec Markets brokerage.
All challenge and funded accounts remain simulated and participants are rewarded on the basis of performance rather than on profits made from trades in the actual market.
