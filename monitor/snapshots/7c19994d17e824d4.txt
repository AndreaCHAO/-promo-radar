As one of the leading international platforms for global financial technology exchange, WikiEXPO, hosted by WikiGlobal and co-organized by WikiFX , WikiBit , WikiLicense , WikiGold , will once again bring together professionals from the financial services, technology, digital assets, regulatory compliance, capital markets, and blockchain sectors to explore the latest trends, opportunities, and challenges shaping the future of financial technology. From regulatory frameworks to digital assets, artificial intelligence to blockchain, capital markets to asset management, and cross-border financial innovation to RegTech, WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 will focus on the profound changes taking place across the global fintech ecosystem. The event will provide an international platform for industry professionals to exchange ideas, build connections, showcase technologies, and explore business opportunities. On November 6, 2026, WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 will officially open in grand style in Limassol, Cyprus. Distinguished Speakers to Gather and Explore the Future of Global Fintech
WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 will welcome numerous industry leaders and professionals from financial services, regulatory compliance, digital assets, blockchain, capital markets, artificial intelligence, and corporate innovation. Professionals from different countries and across a wide range of financial and technology sectors will engage in multidimensional discussions, sharing insights into the latest developments in regulation, investment, trading, digital assets, and technological innovation. More speakers and details about the conference program will be announced in the coming weeks. Stay tuned for the latest official updates. Exploring Emerging Trends and New Opportunities in Fintech
As the global financial system undergoes rapid digital transformation, artificial intelligence, blockchain, digital assets, tokenization, and automation are redefining the boundaries of financial services. WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 will feature in-depth discussions on some of the industry’s most closely watched areas, including: Growth opportunities in emerging-market forex and the challenges of localized compliance New trends in global regulation and AML compliance Digital assets, stablecoins, and blockchain innovation AI-powered financial services and operational automation New opportunities in capital markets, trading, and liquidity The evolution of asset management and the investment ecosystem Web3, tokenization, and cross-border financial innovation Cyprus: A Key Financial Hub Connecting Europe and Global Markets
As an EU member state and an important gateway connecting Europe with the Middle East and North Africa, Cyprus has long held a distinctive position in international finance, investment, corporate services, and technological innovation. Limassol, in particular, is home to a growing community of international financial institutions, investment firms, technology companies, and professional service providers, creating favorable conditions for global fintech companies to engage in cross-border exchange and collaboration. Against this backdrop, WikiEXPO’s return to Cyprus for an international fintech event will further strengthen connections between the European market and the global financial technology ecosystem. From Europe to the Middle East, from traditional finance to digital assets, and from regulatory compliance to technological innovation, Limassol is emerging as an important window into the development of global fintech. More Than an Expo — A Global Convergence of Industry Resources
WikiEXPO is committed to connecting participants across the financial technology ecosystem. For investors, the event provides an opportunity to understand industry trends, explore emerging sectors, and gain valuable market insights. For financial institutions and businesses, it serves as a platform for expanding into international markets, establishing business partnerships, and connecting with industry resources. For technology companies and blockchain projects, it offers a platform to showcase innovative technologies, identify potential partners, and explore opportunities in global markets. For regulatory and compliance professionals, it provides an opportunity to exchange insights on global regulatory practices and discuss how innovation and risk management can be balanced in an evolving financial landscape. For traders, brokers, and asset management institutions, it offers an international platform to understand market developments, exchange trading experience, and explore potential business opportunities. Here, different markets, technologies, and perspectives will come together, creating opportunities for new ideas, connections, and collaborations. The Global Fintech Community Meets in Limassol
On November 6, 2026, WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 will officially open its doors. More than an industry event focused on financial technology, it will serve as an important platform for connecting professionals across global finance, investment, digital assets, and technological innovation. As artificial intelligence meets finance, blockchain moves toward real-world applications, digital assets continue to converge with traditional capital markets, and regulatory frameworks around the world continue to evolve, a new era of finance is accelerating into reality. On November 6, we look forward to gathering in Limassol, Cyprus. Together, we will hear from global industry leaders, explore new directions in financial technology, discover opportunities for cross-border collaboration, and witness the latest developments across the global financial and technology ecosystem. WikiEXPO Cyprus 2026 is almost here. We look forward to welcoming industry partners from around the world in Limassol! Event information and the latest updates will continue to be released through official channels. Global audiences will also be able to follow upcoming livestreams and online interactive activities.
