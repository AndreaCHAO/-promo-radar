FAQs
What is a Forex CPA affiliate?
A forex CPA affiliate is an individual that partners with a forex broker to promote their services and attract new clients. They earn CPA commissions based on the referred clients' account sign-ups or trading activity.
However, if you’re interested in the Vantage Introducing Broker Program, you can find out more here .
How Does Forex CPA Affiliate Program work?
A Forex CPA affiliate program works by allowing individuals to partner up as affiliates for a forex broker. Upon joining as a forex CPA affiliate, you receive a unique affiliate link or tracking code to promote the broker's services. When potential clients click on the affiliate's link and open trading accounts or engage in trading activities, you (the affiliate) earn CPA commissions based on the referred clients' trading volume or account activity. Moreover, brokers typically offer a range of marketing tools to support their affiliates, facilitating their success in attracting and converting potential clients.
