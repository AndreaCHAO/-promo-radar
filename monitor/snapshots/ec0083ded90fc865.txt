The Bonus $111 Promotion Is Concluded: What’s Next?
05.05.2026 • Read: 1 min • Author: Adam Lienhard
For the last three years, the Bonus $111 has been our most popular promotion. It has helped thousands of traders start their journey and earn real profits. Today, we are closing this promotion to make room for something different. Explore how this change will affect you as our trader.
New activations
From today, Bonus $111 is no longer available to join. We have disabled new activations so that we can focus on the new promotion starting very soon.
Rules for current and past users
If you are currently using the bonus, or if you have unclaimed profits in your account, please read these rules carefully:
If you recently activated the bonus, you still have your full 7 days to trade as usual.
Once your 7-day trading week ends, you have 90 days to trade the required lots and withdraw your profits.
If you have bonus profits from the past that you have not withdrawn yet, you also have 90 days from today to meet the requirements and claim them.
After this 90-day period, any unclaimed bonus profits will be removed. We want to give everyone enough time to get their funds, so please plan your trading accordingly.
What is coming next?
We are retiring the $111 because we have developed a new promotion that we believe will be even better for you. We have used everything we learned over the last three years to make this next step more rewarding for our community.
Please check the Headway news and updates regularly. We will reveal the new promotion next week. Thank you for being a valued member of the Headway community.
