What is '500Affiliates'?
500Affiliates™ is the official affiliate’s program of the global Plus500™ trading platform, and is considered to be one of the largest and most profitable affiliate programs in the financial market.
Who can join?
Companies and private persons above the age of 18.
Does it cost to be a member of 500Affiliates?
There are no costs or monthly fees to be a member at +500Affiliates™ program. It is completely free!
Do I need to have a website in order to promote Plus500 as an affiliate?
Yes. Plus500 is a regulated company and due to these requirements, our affiliates are only authorized to promote Plus500 on websites which are approved by us. This actually proves our seriousness and transparent policy.
How do I get my site approved?
Log in to your affiliate account in order to access our Marketing Rules , which state the “Do’s and Don’ts” while promoting Plus500.
Choose from our variety of Marketing Tools (banners, widgets, logos etc.) and implement them to your website.
Submit your website for our review in your affiliate account
Once your site is approved by our compliance team, the affiliate links will be activated and you may begin referring traffic to Plus500!
Who is a Real Trader?
A Real Trader is a user that opened a trading account and made a first deposit.
