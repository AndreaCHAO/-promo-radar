Introducing Broker Program
Are there any costs or fees associated with becoming an IB?
No, becoming an IB is completely free.
That said, if you choose to run your own promotional activities or offer additional services you offer to your clients, those may involve associated costs.
Do you offer a Multi-tier model?
Yes, we offer a Multi-tier model. Please contact your Tickmill Partnership Manager or email us at support@tickmill.com, and we’ll guide you through the setup.
Do you offer an IB Bonus Program? How can I join?
Yes, Tickmill offers an IB Bonus Program designed to reward Introducing Brokers (IBs) for their referral efforts. To join, simply register as an IB using this link .
How and when are IB commissions added to my account?
Whenever your referred client makes a trade, the commission is automatically calculated and shown in your IB Room.
IB commissions are credited to your IB balance every weekend and become available for withdrawal at that point.
How can I unlock the “Claim Reward” button?
The Get Prize button becomes available once the IB commission check is completed. This process can take up to 5 business days from the moment the commission is added to your wallet.
How can I withdraw my IB commissions?
You can withdraw your IB commissions at any time by submitting a withdrawal request through your Client Area.
Funds can be:
Transferred to your Tickmill live trading account, or
Withdrawn to your bank account, e-wallet, or any other supported payment method.
How do I register for an Introducing Broker (IB) account?
To become an IB at Tickmill, follow these steps:
Create a Client Area account *.
Verify your profile
Create your IB account
Access your referral links and start earning commission
*If you are an existing client, please insert your account number.
How do IB commissions work?
IBs earn commissions based on the trading volume generated by the clients they refer to Tickmill. The commission structure is typically tiered, and rates may vary depending on client activity or account type.
How much commission can I earn as an Introducing Broker (IB)?
You can earn commissions in multiple ways:
- Up to 55% of the spread on Classic accounts.
- $2.5 per lot (round turn) on RAW accounts.
- 5% commission discount for your clients on MT5 RAW accounts.
Learn more here .
Is there a minimum number of clients I need to refer to earn commissions?
No, there’s no minimum. You start earning commissions even if you refer just one client. Every trade they place counts. Of course, the more clients you refer, the greater your earning potential.
Please check the IB Terms & Conditions in your Client Area for full details.
What are the benefits of Tickmill's IB program?
Our IB Program offers a unique opportunity to earn income without any upfront investment. As an IB, you gain access to a range of valuable benefits, including:
High commission rates.
A loyalty program with cash prizes.
Dedicated account management.
A comprehensive reporting dashboard to track your earnings and client referrals.
Special marketing tools to help you attract new clients easily and effectively.
No limits on the duration, number, or volume of trades.
No restrictions on the difference between open and close prices.
No cap on the number of new clients you can refer.
Thanks to Tickmill’s transparent tracking system, you can monitor every client and trade that generates commission for you, giving you full visibility and control over your performance.
