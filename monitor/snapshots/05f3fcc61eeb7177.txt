Key information about Trading Credit
• You take part only if you choose to join the Campaign, and you can leave at any time (Clause 3.10).
• Trading Credit is not money. You cannot withdraw it, and it does not absorb losses.
• Trading Credit lets you open larger positions than your own funds alone would support. This can increase your losses and how quickly your account reaches the Stop Out Level.
• Stop Out is calculated on your own funds (Real Equity) only. Your positions can be closed while the trading platform still shows an equity figure that includes Trading Credit (see the example in Clause 5.9).
• Trading Credit is removed in full on Stop Out, reduced when you withdraw or transfer funds, and expires on 31 March 2027.
• Trading CFDs involves a high level of risk. You may lose all the money you deposit.

2. Definitions
For the purposes of the Campaign:
“Eligible Client” means a Client who:
a. has completed IUX’s customer due diligence, which is up to date when the Campaign Consent is given;
b. holds an Eligible Account;
c. resides in an Eligible Jurisdiction;
d. is at least eighteen (18) years old and of legal age in the Client’s jurisdiction; and
e. is not restricted, suspended or disqualified by IUX.
“Campaign Consent” has the meaning given in Clause 1.4.
“Eligible Account” means a trading account of a type designated by IUX as eligible to participate in the Campaign and published on the IUX website.
“Eligible Jurisdiction” means a jurisdiction listed as eligible on the Campaign page of the IUX website.
“Qualifying Deposit” means a deposit that is successfully processed and credited to an Eligible Account during the Campaign Period, in accordance with the terms, conditions and arrangements governing accounts, deposits and payment processing as displayed in the IUX system.
“First Qualifying Deposit” means the first Qualifying Deposit credited after the Client has given the Campaign Consent, subject to Clause 1.10.
“Subsequent Qualifying Deposit” means each Qualifying Deposit credited after the First Qualifying Deposit.
“Trading Credit” means non-withdrawable promotional credit granted to an Eligible Account under the Campaign.
“Real Balance” means that part of the account Balance which is derived solely from the Client’s deposited funds and realised trading profit or loss, excluding the value of any Trading Credit.
“Real Equity” means the Real Balance plus or minus unrealised profit or loss on open positions, excluding Trading Credit. It is the sole basis used for the calculation of the Margin Level and Stop Out under Clause 5.8.
“Platform Equity” means the Real Balance plus Trading Credit, plus or minus unrealised profit or loss. It is the equity figure displayed by the trading platform by default and is not used for the calculation of Stop Out.
“Margin Level” means the ratio of Real Equity to the margin used, multiplied by one hundred.
“Margin Call Level” and “Stop Out Level” mean the levels of Margin Level, determined and published by IUX, at which the system issues a warning and at which the system begins to close positions automatically, respectively.
“Order Execution Policy” means the IUX Order Execution Policy as in force from time to time.
“Campaign Cap” means the maximum cumulative Trading Credit that may be granted to one Client under the Campaign, as set out in Clause 4.4.
“Balance Before Transaction” means the Balance recorded in the Eligible Account immediately before a withdrawal or internal transfer is processed, which includes deposited funds and realised profit or loss but excludes Trading Credit and unrealised profit or loss on open positions.
“Linked Accounts” means two or more accounts which IUX has reasonable grounds, based on objective indicators such as identity verification documents, devices, IP addresses, payment channels or contact details (supported, where relevant, by patterns of deposits, withdrawals and trading), to believe are controlled by the same person or by persons acting together.
“Third-Party Money Manager” means any service or account operating as a Fund Manager, MAM or PAMM, any account receiving commercial trading signals, and any account managed by a person other than the account holder.
“Manifest Error” means an obvious error, including a price from a liquidity provider or price source that is materially divergent from the prevailing market price at the relevant time, an incorrect calculation of Trading Credit, or the duplicate granting of Trading Credit.
“Trading Server Time” means the time of the IUX trading server, as shown on the trading platform.

4. Calculation of Trading Credit
4.1 An Eligible Client who has given the Campaign Consent may receive Trading Credit equal to 100% of the First Qualifying Deposit, up to a maximum of USD 500 in Trading Credit.
4.2 Having received Trading Credit under Clause 4.1, the Client may receive Trading Credit equal to 50% of each Subsequent Qualifying Deposit.
4.3 Trading Credit granted under Clause 4.2 is cumulative and is subject to a further maximum of USD 1,000.
4.4 The total Trading Credit that may be granted to one Client under the (the Campaign Cap) shall not exceed USD 1,500, regardless of the number or amount of Qualifying Deposits made by the Client. It comprises:
a. up to USD 500 under the 100% Trading Credit stage for the First Qualifying Deposit, determined solely by the amount of that deposit. Any portion of the USD 500 that is not used is not carried forward, accumulated or transferred to the maximum available under Clauses 4.2 and 4.3. For example, a First Qualifying Deposit of USD 200 attracts Trading Credit of USD 200, and the unused USD 300 is not carried forward; and
b. up to USD 1,000 under the 50% Trading Credit stage.
4.5 The Campaign Period ends on 31 January 2027 at 23:59:59 (Trading Server Time). Deposits credited after that time do not receive Trading Credit, even if initiated before it. Clients should allow for payment processing times, which may be longer at weekends and on public holidays. The Campaign does not renew automatically.
4.6 The Campaign Cap is calculated on the basis of the aggregate Trading Credit previously granted to the Client, including Trading Credit granted under the previous version of these Terms and Conditions.
4.7 Trading Credit that has been reduced, suspended, withdrawn, removed, cancelled or has expired continues to count towards the Campaign Cap and does not restore any entitlement already used.
4.8 Internal transfers, refunds, reversed transactions, chargebacks, payment corrections and any other transactions not treated by the IUX system as a successful deposit do not qualify for Trading Credit.
4.9 Trading Credit is calculated on the amount of the Qualifying Deposit credited to the Eligible Account, as recorded in the IUX system.
4.10 Trading Credit will ordinarily be granted after the Qualifying Deposit has been successfully processed. Delays may occur as a result of technical checks, payment verification, compliance review or risk controls.
4.11 Campaign budget cap: IUX may set an aggregate maximum amount of Trading Credit for the Campaign. Once the Trading Credit granted reaches that amount, IUX may suspend the granting of further Trading Credit or end the Campaign in accordance with Clause 10, without affecting Trading Credit already granted to Clients. IUX will publish a notice when it suspends the granting of further Trading Credit on this basis. Qualifying Deposits credited before the notice is published will still receive Trading Credit.
4.12 Where IUX identifies a Manifest Error in the granting of Trading Credit, IUX may correct the amount of Trading Credit, giving reasons to the Client. IUX will not rely on this Clause to cancel profits made in good faith by the Client before the correction, save to the extent that such profits arise directly from the excess Trading Credit granted.
4.13 The value of Trading Credit is fixed in the currency of the Eligible Account at the time it is granted and is not recalculated by reference to subsequent movements in exchange rates. Where an Eligible Account is not denominated in USD, the USD amounts in this Clause 4 are applied using the exchange rate used by IUX at the time the Trading Credit is granted.
5. Use of Trading Credit
5.1 Trading Credit may be used only as additional margin support for trading on an Eligible Account.
5.2 Trading Credit may not be:
a. withdrawn as cash;
b. transferred to another trading account, wallet or Client, except to another Eligible Account of the same Client under Clause 6.2(b);
c. exchanged or converted into cash; or
d. used outside the Eligible Account to which it was granted or transferred under Clause 6.2(b).
5.3 Profits generated from trading may be withdrawn, subject to the Client Agreement, the withdrawal requirements, the margin requirements, compliance checks and these Terms and Conditions.
5.4 Trading Credit does not guarantee that the Client will be able to open or maintain any particular position and does not protect the Client against trading losses.
5.5 Stop Out and margin calculations are determined in accordance with the applicable IUX trading system and margin rules, as described in Clause 5.8. Trading Credit is not included in the account value used by the system to determine Stop Out.
5.6 The reduction, withdrawal, removal, cancellation or expiry of Trading Credit may reduce available margin and may result in a Margin Call, a Stop Out or the automatic closure of open positions.
5.7 The Client is responsible for monitoring the account, open positions, the Margin Level and the amount of Trading Credit available.
5.8 Two separate calculation bases apply: (a) the margin check performed when a new position is opened includes Trading Credit as available margin; and (b) Margin Level and Stop Out are calculated on Real Equity as the sole basis, excluding Trading Credit. IUX may decline to open a new position where, after opening, the Margin Level would be below the Margin Call Level.
5.9 It follows from Clause 5.8 that a Client’s positions may be closed by the Stop Out mechanism while Platform Equity and Trading Credit remain in the account. The trading platform displays Platform Equity, which combines Real Balance and Trading Credit, by default. Trading Credit increases the capacity to open positions but does not bear losses in place of Real Equity and does not defer the Stop Out Level. IUX displays Platform Equity and Real Equity, including the Margin Level used for the calculation of Stop Out, in the Client interface.
Example (illustrative only): a Client has a Real Balance of USD 100 and Trading Credit of USD 100, so Platform Equity is USD 200. Because the opening check includes Trading Credit, the Client may open positions requiring margin of USD 150. The Margin Level is then 66.7% (Real Equity USD 100 ÷ margin USD 150 × 100). If the Stop Out Level were 30%, a loss of USD 55 would trigger Stop Out (Real Equity USD 45), although the platform would still display Platform Equity of USD 145. All remaining Trading Credit would then be removed under Clause 7.1. The Stop Out Level that applies is published on the IUX website.
5.10 Minimum holding period: while an Eligible Account holds Trading Credit, each position opened on that account must be held for not less than five (5) minutes, calculated from the time of opening to the time of closing. This does not apply to positions closed by Stop Out or by IUX.
5.11 Where a Client repeatedly closes positions before the period in Clause 5.10 has elapsed, IUX may warn the Client in writing. After the warning, IUX may decline to treat profits from positions closed before that period has elapsed as withdrawable profits and may take measures under Clause 9.4. IUX will assess the Client’s overall pattern of conduct rather than any individual transaction, and will give the warning before applying any measure affecting the Client’s entitlements, save where there is a clear risk to the integrity of IUX’s systems.
5.12 All charges, including swap charges, commissions, payment fees and any other fees, are deducted from Real Equity only. Trading Credit may not be used to settle charges.
5.13 Negative Balance Protection under the Client Agreement continues to apply. Where a negative balance results from fraud or abuse described in Clause 9.2(a), (b), ( c ), (d), (h), (k), (m) or (p), IUX may recover that amount from the Client as permitted by the Client Agreement. 5.16 Upon reaching the Stop Out Level, the system closes positions automatically, beginning with the most loss-making position, until the Margin Level returns within the required threshold. A Stop Out takes precedence over all other orders.
6. Withdrawals and Internal Transfers
6.1 A Client may request a withdrawal or an internal transfer from an Eligible Account, subject to the ordinary IUX withdrawal, transfer, compliance and margin requirements.
6.2 Where funds are withdrawn or internally transferred out of an Eligible Account:
a. if funds are withdrawn, Trading Credit is reduced in the same proportion as the amount withdrawn bears to the Balance Before Transaction;
b. if funds are transferred to another Eligible Account of the same Client, Trading Credit is transferred to that account in the same proportion as the amount transferred bears to the Balance Before Transaction; and
c. if funds are transferred to any other account, including a Raw or Pro account, Trading Credit is reduced in the same proportion as the amount transferred bears to the Balance Before Transaction.
The applicable calculation is:
Withdrawal Ratio = Amount Deducted ÷ Balance Before Transaction
Trading Credit Deduction = Trading Credit Before Transaction × Withdrawal Ratio
6.3 Worked example:
Before the withdrawal — Balance Before Transaction: USD 500; Trading Credit: USD 300; withdrawal amount: USD 100.
The Withdrawal Ratio is USD 100 ÷ USD 500 = 20%.
The Trading Credit Deduction is USD 300 × 20% = USD 60.
After the transaction — remaining Balance: USD 400; remaining Trading Credit: USD 240.
6.4 If the Client withdraws the entire Balance of an Eligible Account, or transfers it to an account that is not an Eligible Account, all remaining Trading Credit in that account is removed. If the entire Balance is transferred to another Eligible Account of the same Client, the Trading Credit is transferred in accordance with Clause 6.2(b).
6.5 Trading Credit is reduced or transferred when the withdrawal or internal transfer is processed, using the calculation in Clause 6.2 at that time.
6.6 Prior to confirming a withdrawal or internal transfer, IUX displays a preview of the Trading Credit amount to be deducted or transferred and the remaining Trading Credit after the transaction. The Client is responsible for maintaining sufficient margin before requesting a withdrawal or internal transfer. The reduction of Trading Credit may affect open positions and may result in a Margin Call, a Stop Out or the automatic closure of positions.
6.7 If a deposit is refunded, reversed, cancelled, charged back or otherwise invalidated, IUX may recalculate or withdraw any Trading Credit granted in connection with that deposit.
7. Stop Out and Trading Credit Reset
7.1 If a Stop Out occurs on an Eligible Account, all remaining Trading Credit in that account is removed.
7.2 Trading Credit removed following a Stop Out is not reinstated automatically.
7.3 Trading Credit granted before the Stop Out continues to count towards the Client’s Campaign Cap.
7.4 Where the Campaign remains in force and the Client remains eligible, a future Qualifying Deposit may attract Trading Credit only from such Campaign entitlement as has not previously been granted.
7.5 A Stop Out, the removal of Trading Credit or the closure of positions does not create a new 100% Trading Credit entitlement and does not reset the Campaign Cap.

8.3 Expired Trading Credit continues to count towards the Client’s Campaign Cap.

8.6 IUX will notify the Client in writing not less than seven (7) days before Trading Credit is removed under Clause 8.1, through the Client’s Personal Area and registered email address.
9. Campaign Abuse and Prohibited Conduct
9.1 The Campaign is intended for genuine trading activity. Clients must not use the Campaign to obtain an unfair or artificially created benefit.
9.2 Prohibited techniques and conduct include, but are not limited to:
a. creating duplicate or multiple Client profiles, or using Linked Accounts to circumvent the Campaign Cap;
b. using false information, the identity of another person, or payment channels that are unauthorised or do not belong to the Client;
c. opening opposing positions or coordinated hedging between related accounts or together with other parties;
d. transferring, recycling or repeatedly depositing and withdrawing funds principally in order to obtain Trading Credit;
e. conducting artificial trading activity with no genuine market exposure, including trading intended to generate volume in order to obtain a rebate;
f. exploiting price errors, off-market prices, price or feed latency, execution delays, platform errors or system vulnerabilities;
g. latency arbitrage; using expert advisors or algorithmic strategies designed to exploit weaknesses in IUX’s pricing, execution or platform; using external price feeds to exploit delays in IUX’s prices; and submitting orders in volumes affecting the stability of the system;
h. using Trading Credit, alone or together with Linked Accounts or other persons, in a way designed to shift losses to IUX, including through Negative Balance Protection;
i. trading on the basis of inside information concerning price movements, or using Linked Accounts or opposing positions to hold exposure funded by Trading Credit across scheduled events or market gaps;
j. using the Client’s account for the benefit of another person, managing funds on behalf of another person, or receiving commercial trading signals on an account holding Trading Credit;
k. reversing or charging back a deposit after Trading Credit has been received;
l. any other conduct intended to obtain Trading Credit or profits through a breach of these Terms and Conditions or by manipulating the Campaign or the IUX trading system;
m. swap manipulation, including holding opposing positions across accounts in order to capture the swap differential;
n. submitting order flow designed to profit from delays or weaknesses in IUX’s pricing or liquidity arrangements rather than from genuine market risk (toxic flow);
o. rebate arbitrage, including generating trading volume so that the rebate received exceeds the losses accepted;
p. incurring systematic losses on an account while generating partner remuneration for a Linked Account or a linked person;
q. using multi-account management tools, such as MT5 Multiterminal, APIs or copy trading systems, to manage accounts that do not belong to the Client;
r. using software that submits orders in a volume or at a frequency which affects server stability or which prevents IUX from executing the orders of other Clients at ordinary standards of quality.
9.3 IUX will determine whether Clause 9.2 has been breached by reference to its system records, including the transaction history, deposit and withdrawal history, trading activities, account activities and other relevant information. IUX will not exercise its discretion without supporting evidence from its system records and will give the Client a summary of the evidence relied upon, unless prohibited by law. IUX is not required to disclose its detection methods, its security controls or the personal data of other persons.
9.4 Subject to applicable law and to the Client Agreement, and acting proportionately to the nature and seriousness of the breach, IUX may take one or more of the following actions:
a. withhold, reduce, withdraw or cancel Trading Credit;
b. cancel profits or benefits arising from the prohibited conduct;
c. reverse the affected transactions;
d. suspend or terminate participation in the Campaign;
e. restrict, suspend or close the relevant account in accordance with the Client Agreement; and
f. recover losses, costs or benefits obtained through the prohibited conduct.
The Client’s deposited funds, less any amount recoverable under this Clause, remain available for withdrawal, subject to the Client Agreement and applicable law.
9.5 During an investigation, IUX may temporarily suspend or delay the granting of Trading Credit, withdrawals, transfers or other affected transactions, to the extent reasonably necessary and subject to Clause 13.3.
9.6 Before taking any measure under Clause 9.4, IUX will notify the Client of the conduct suspected and the measure proposed, and will give the Client at least five (5) business days to respond, except where (a) disclosure is prohibited by law, (b) there is a clear risk to the integrity of IUX’s systems, or ( c ) an interim suspension is needed under Clause 13. The Client may challenge any measure under Clause 11.2.
