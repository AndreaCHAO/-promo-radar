Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a verified account to participate?
Yes. Only verified accounts (with a completed KYC) are eligible. Most campaigns also require clients to manually opt in via the web or the client portal in the app.
When will I receive my reward?
Rewards are typically issued after the promotion ends and eligibility is confirmed, and may take up to 90 days. Physical gifts will be delivered to the address provided, while bonus rewards will be credited to the voucher portal in the app.
Can rewards be withdrawn?
This depends on the reward type. Trading rewards, vouchers, and cash rewards may have different usage and withdrawal rules. Some rewards may only be available after specific conditions are met, as stated in the Terms and Conditions.
Where can I find the full Terms and Conditions?
The full Terms and Conditions can be found on the promotion landing page or in the app after you opt in. Look for “T&C Apply”.
Who can I contact for help?
Please contact our support team at info@ultimamarkets.com
