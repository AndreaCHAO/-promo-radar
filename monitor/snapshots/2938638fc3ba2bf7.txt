How can I apply to become an Introducing Broker (IB)?
Step-by-step guide for new and existing clients to apply to become an Introducing Broker (IB), including eligibility, the MT5 rebate account, and what happens after applying. Updated 14 days ago
Both new and existing clients can apply to become an Introducing Broker (IB) with Axi.
For new clients
Go to the Introducing Broker Program page .
Click “Sign Up Now”
You are redirected to the full application. Provide your trading experience, expected clients, your details and your account preferences (such as currency).
Complete the prompted steps.
For existing Axi clients
Log into your Client Portal, navigate to the “Partners” tab, choose your preferred partnership type and complete the prompted steps.
Eligibility
You must be 18 or older.
You must be from a permitted country. Axi is not onboarding new partners from prohibited countries at this time.
Individual clients can apply. Corporate or joint accounts are not eligible at the moment.
What happens after I apply
Rebate account: your rebate account is created on MT5. IB Portal access can take up to 24 hours; you will receive an email with your credentials.
Client Portal: you access it using your Auth0 login (email and password, Google, Apple).
Verification: complete the required identity verification before you can withdraw rebates.
Trading account: you can also request a trading account directly in the Client Portal.
