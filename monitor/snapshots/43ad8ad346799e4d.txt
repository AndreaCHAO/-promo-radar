Withdrawal conditions: Affiliate Program
1108 views 11 September 5, 2023
To ensure that the withdrawal of affiliate commissions is processed efficiently, transparently, and in compliance with security regulations, affiliates are required to follow the conditions below:
Commission Withdrawal Limits
Affiliates may request commission withdrawals within a range of 10 USD (minimum) to 10,000 USD (maximum) per transaction.
Bank Account Name Verification
The name on the bank account used for commission withdrawals must match the name provided in the verified Proof of Identity (POI). If there is any discrepancy or mismatch, the withdrawal request may not be processed. This requirement is in place to prevent fraud and to ensure maximum security in the transfer of funds to affiliates.
