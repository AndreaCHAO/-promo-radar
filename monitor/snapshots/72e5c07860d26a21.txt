Terms and conditions
To participate in the Lucky Post, all participants will need to sign up, repost the action and share the link in the support chat.
The winners will be chosen by accident.
Lucky Post tickets will be reset to zero daily and requests will close daily at 23:59 (+02:00).
The daily Lucky Post draw for the previous day will start at 00:00 (+02:00).
Requests submitted on Saturday and Sunday, will be selected and played next Monday.
Participants will only be able to win one award per day, but may also win the next day. If the user wins the main prize, then he/she cannot win the mini-prize, and vice versa.
Distribution of rewards: Rewards will be distributed instantly at the time of summing up of results of the draw.
Closed transactions only are taken into account.
The total volume of closed positions are not taken into account:
– positions made on the account before participation
– positions made on bonus funds
