Be informed: risks of trading with forex bonuses
Forex bonuses can increase available trading capital, but they also introduce additional risks that traders should fully understand before participating.
Market volatility can lead to rapid losses
The forex market is highly volatile and even when trading with a bonus, losses can occur just as quickly as gains. Monitor your positions and cultivate healthy risk management behaviours.
Leveraged trading increases risk exposure
When holding leveraged positions during periods of low liquidity or high volatility, traders should consider using lower leverage ratios, setting stop-loss orders, maintaining sufficient margin balances, and closely monitoring the market conditions.
Bonus terms and withdrawal restrictions
Forex bonuses often come with conditions such as minimum trading volume requirements, time limitations, or restrictions on withdrawals. Ensure you are comfortable with them and avoid exposing yourself to unnecessary risks.
