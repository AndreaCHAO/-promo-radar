FAQ
Questions traders actually ask
Is TNFX a regulated broker?
Yes. TNFX Ltd is authorised as a Securities Dealer by the Financial Services Authority (FSA) of Seychelles under licence number SD133. TNFX Markets Ltd is registered in St. Vincent & the Grenadines (No. 27346). Full legal details are in the footer below.
Are TNFX trading accounts swap-free?
Swap-free (Islamic) status is available on request on any of the five TNFX account types — Standard, Cent, Turbo, Zero and VIP. It is applied to your individual trading account rather than granted automatically by tier, so confirm it is active on your account number before holding a position overnight.
What is the maximum leverage at TNFX?
Up to 1:3000 on forex and 1:1000 on gold, on the Turbo account. Turbo leverage is dynamic — it is derived from account equity and steps down as the account grows: 1:3000 under $1,000 equity, 1:2000 from $1,000, 1:1000 from $3,000, 1:500 from $5,000, 1:300 from $50,000 and 1:100 above $100,000. Standard, Cent and Zero are fixed at 1:500 and VIP at 1:100. Indices and energy are capped at 1:100 on every account type.
What is the maximum leverage on gold?
1:1000, on the Turbo account, while account equity is under $1,000. TNFX does not offer 1:2000 or 1:3000 on gold — those figures apply to forex only. Gold leverage steps down with equity in the same way and reaches 1:100 above $100,000.
What is the minimum deposit?
The Standard and Cent accounts start from $100 . The Zero account — with EURUSD spreads from 0.0 pips and a $5 per-lot commission — starts from $500 .
Which platforms can I trade on?
MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 on Windows, iOS and Android, plus the browser-based Web Terminal . One login works across all your devices.
How do I deposit and withdraw money?
Through the secure Traders Room , using more than a dozen methods — including Neteller, Skrill, PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer, Sticpay, Payeer, WebMoney and local wallets like Sham Cash.
How do I become a TNFX partner (IB)?
Register at the partner portal in minutes. Partners earn up to $10 per traded lot from referred clients, with weekly cash challenges, a multi-level sub-IB network and real-time reporting.
