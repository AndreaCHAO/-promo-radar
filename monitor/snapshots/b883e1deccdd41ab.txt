The countdown to Finance Magnates Summit London 2026 continues. On 24 November 2026, the financial services industry will return to Old Billingsgate for the Summit’s 15th edition, bringing together senior decision-makers, industry leaders and professionals for a focused day of networking, knowledge-sharing and business development.
Representatives from Institutional Trading, Technology Providers, Retail Brokers, Fintech and Payments will come together to strengthen professional relationships, explore new opportunities and discuss the developments shaping their industry.
For those looking to expand their networks, reconnect with existing partners and discover new business opportunities, the Summit offers a dedicated environment to make meaningful connections.
Make the Most of Your Experience in London
Meet Your Next Business Partner
The Summit brings together C-level executives, founders, directors and senior professionals from across financial services, creating opportunities to connect face-to-face with the people driving the industry forward.
Whether the goal is to establish new partnerships, explore potential collaborations or strengthen existing business relationships, these conversations can be an important starting point for future opportunities.
Hear What's Shaping the Industry
The financial services landscape continues to evolve, creating new challenges and opportunities for businesses across the sector.
Through industry discussions and expert debates, Finance Magnates Summit London 2026 will provide a platform to explore current developments, exchange perspectives and gain valuable industry intelligence.
Attendees can engage with the topics influencing their markets and take away insights relevant to their business strategies.
Turn Introductions Into Opportunities With Business Connect
Building connections is only the beginning. Business Connect helps attendees make the most of their time at the Summit by facilitating relevant business conversations.
From meeting prospective clients to reconnecting with existing partners, attendees can use Business Connect to identify valuable contacts and arrange conversations aligned with their professional goals.
With the right people in one place, the Summit creates opportunities to turn introductions into meaningful business relationships.
Start Connecting Before the Summit
Networking begins one day before the main event with the Opening Networking Blitz at The Folly on 23 November 2026.
The pre-Summit gathering gives attendees an opportunity to meet fellow professionals, establish initial connections and begin their Summit experience ahead of the main event at Old Billingsgate.
It is an opportunity to start conversations early and make the most of the time spent in London.
One Focused Day. New Connections. New Opportunities.
As Finance Magnates Summit London celebrates its 15th edition, the focus remains on bringing together the people, companies and conversations that matter to the financial services industry.
From face-to-face networking and industry discussions to targeted business meetings, the Summit offers one dedicated day to strengthen relationships, exchange knowledge and explore what comes next.
With 24 November approaching, now is the time to plan your visit and secure your place among the professionals gathering in London.
