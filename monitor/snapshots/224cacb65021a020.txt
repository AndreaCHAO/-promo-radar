Frequently Asked Questions
What is an Introducing Broker (IB)?
An introducing broker (IB) is a trusted partner that connects clients to TMGM and earns commissions based on their trading activity. IBs leverage their network and expertise to earn volume-based commissions on every trade their referred clients make through the IB program .
How do I become an Introducing Broker (IB)?
Becoming an introducing broker is simple:
1. Apply via the TMGM Partners website and join the IB program
2. Receive your personalized IB broker dashboard and referral link
3. Promote TMGM using your network and our marketing tools
4. Earn daily commissions from your clients’ trading activity
Everything is tracked in real time, so you can focus on growing your business.
What are the benefits of being an Introducing Broker (IB)?
Joining the IB program comes with powerful benefits:
High Earnings : Earn up to $10 per standard lot traded
Fast Payouts : Daily withdrawals with no minimums
Sub-IB Network : Build your team and increase your revenue
Dedicated Support : Work with a personal IB broker account manager
VIP Access : Attend exclusive events, like Chelsea FC match experiences
Marketing Tools : Get custom-made assets designed for IB broker growth
Are there any requirements to become an Introducing Broker (IB)?
To join the IB program , you should have basic trading knowledge and a network of potential clients. It’s ideal for financial professionals, educators, and market influencers. As an IB broker , you’re expected to support your clients, follow industry standards, and comply with regulations. No license is required to get started, but professionalism is a must.
How are my rewards calculated?
As an introducing broker , your rewards are based on the total trading volume (measured in lots) of your referred clients. The IB program offers tiered commission rates, up to $10 per lot, with real-time tracking through your IB broker dashboard. Commissions are paid daily, providing you with quick and transparent access to your earnings.
