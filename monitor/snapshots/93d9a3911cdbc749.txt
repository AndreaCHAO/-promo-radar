Meet the FxPro Team at The Trading Festival in Mexico City
2026-11-02T12:18:17.472+02:00
M o n d a y , 0 2 / 1 1 / 2 0 2 6 | 1 0 : 1 8 G M T by
FxPro Disclaimer
Competitive trading conditions and partnerships take centre stage at Booth #10
Thought Leadership
fxpro
FxPro, a world’s #1 broker, is heading to The Trading Festival Mexico 2026 to connect with its trader and partner base in Mexico. Its priority is clear: to offer its client base more competitive conditions, greater platform choice and support from people who understand the market and its needs.
The broker, who is an Elite sponsor of the two-day event in Mexico City, will be at Booth #10. Its local team is keen to meet with existing and potential clients, IBs, and affiliates to discuss their goals and needs and how the broker can support them.
Mexico is an important country for the broker, and its approach combines a modern trading infrastructure with practical features for the local market, like a trading account in MXN and access to the USDMXN pair.
More Choice for Traders in Mexico
Trading costs, execution and a variety of platforms can make a substantial difference to traders. FxPro offers several account types and platforms so that traders can choose conditions based on their trading preferences.
Clients can trade more than 2,100 instruments through forex, shares, indices, metals, energies and cryptocurrency CFDs. Platform options include MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5 and cTrader with TradingView connectivity. The broker’s five-star-rated trading app features Top Movers and Most Traded in the Discover Hub, showing where trading activity is, while Market Sentiment shows how other traders are positioned. The Raw+ account offers spreads from 0, while the new Nano account allows traders to start with smaller amounts and positions and test their strategies under live market conditions.
The broker also supports local deposit methods in pesos with zero fees, reducing the need for clients to use foreign-currency payment options and incur conversion fees.
Building Long-Term Relationships with Partners
FxPro is also placing greater emphasis on its relationship with IBs and affiliates. A local team is ready to assist partners, which makes day-to-day communication more direct and provides support that takes regional business needs into account.
The Trading Festival Mexico 2026 gives existing partners the chance to discuss their business with the team. Prospective IBs and affiliates can learn about the broker’s Partner Programme, benefits and rewards available to them.
FxPro has been operating since 1999 and has received more than 150 international industry awards, bringing an established track record to the region.
FxPro joins the Fintech Discussion
On the second day of the event, Katherine Jimenez, FxPro’s Business Development Manager in the LATAM region, will join the panel “It’s Show Time: How Fintech Is Changing the Way You Trade” at the Traders Arena, starting at noon. The discussion will examine how technology is changing the trading experience and what traders can expect in 2027.
“We see plenty of opportunities to do more here in Mexico,” said Katherine Jimenez. “Our focus is on understanding what traders and partners need, what’s working for them, offering conditions and services that make sense for their market, and being available when they need us. Events like this give us the chance to meet our clients and have those conversations in person.”
Attendees can visit FxPro at Booth #10 to meet the team and discuss trading and partnership opportunities.
About FxPro
FxPro is the world’s #1 broker with over 150 awards and is trusted by millions of clients worldwide. For almost three decades, the company has provided access to over 2,100 trading instruments across FX, stocks, futures, indices, metals, energy and more via its powerful platforms. Regulated across multiple jurisdictions, FxPro delivers ultra-fast execution, deep liquidity and a client-centric trading experience.
For more information, visit www.fxpro.com

Banking delays, rejected applications and payment disruptions can put trading firms under serious pressure. For businesses operating across multiple jurisdictions and complex corporate structures, having the right banking setup is essential to keeping operations running.
In this Finance Magnates webinar, Avner Primor, Director of Business Development at Clarity Global Inc., explores the banking and payment challenges facing offshore trading businesses in 2026 and what firms can do to prepare.
The discussion looks at how banks assess trading businesses, why banking applications can be rejected, and how companies can improve the way they manage incoming and outgoing payments.
In this webinar, we cover:
Banking and payment developments affecting offshore businesses in 2026.
Common challenges facing CFD brokers, prop trading firms, iGaming operators and crypto businesses.
How banks assess payment flows, customer funds and multi-entity corporate structures.
Two real-world case studies involving banking arrangements for CFD brokers.
What to include in a banking readiness pack before approaching providers.
What to look for when choosing a banking and payment partner.
Why businesses should maintain backup banking providers and alternative payment routes.
What to expect from banking, payments and regulatory developments in 2027.
The session also includes a live Q&A covering onboarding requirements, banking services and crypto transactions.
