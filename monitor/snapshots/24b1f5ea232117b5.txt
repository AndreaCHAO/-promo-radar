Frequently Asked Questions
How do I get 13% p.a. on my account balance?
13% p.a. funds growth is based on a weekly interest calculation of 0.25% per week on eligible funds. The interest settles every Monday (for the previous week) and will be credited to your participation account every 4 weeks, resulting in a total of 13% annually.
Can I participate with multiple accounts?
Yes, you are allowed to participate with multiple accounts. However, each participation account must meet the requirements respectively. For instance, if you participate with two accounts (account A and account B) and account A meets the criteria while account B does not, you will receive the reward associated with account A only and your Eligible Fund and trading volume made on account B will not be combined with account A to calculate your reward.
What is Eligible Fund and how is it calculated for rewards?
Eligible Fund is defined as your account equity subtracted by any credit bonuses in the participation account at 5:30 AM MT Server time every day. For instance, if your equity is USD 6,000 with a credit of USD 500, your rewards will be calculated from USD 5,500.
Please note that if your equity is USD 5,000 with a credit of USD 500, no reward will be accrued as your Eligible Fund (USD 4,500) does not meet the minimum requirement (USD 5,000).
Why is my actual interest payout lower than the projected value shown on the Client Portal?
The actual interest payout is contingent on specific conditions: In order to receive the full interest, it is necessary to maintain eligible funds of at least USD 5,000 daily and execute a minimum of 1 standard lot per USD 5,000 in eligible funds during the settlement week. Failure to meet these criteria may result in a lower/no interest payout.
[Example A] If a client completes 1 standard lot of trading and maintains his/her eligible funds of USD 18,000 during the settlement week. He/she will receive a payout of USD 12.5 (USD 5,000 x 0.25%) for this settlement week.
[Example B] If a client completes 2 standard lots of trading and maintains his/her eligible funds of USD 18,000 during the settlement week. He/she will receive a payout of USD 25 (USD 10,000 x 0.25%) for this settlement week.
[Example C] If a client completes 2 standard lots of trading and maintains his/her eligible funds of USD 8,000 during the settlement week. He/she will receive a payout of USD 20 (USD 8,000 x 0.25%) for this settlement week.
Here are some more examples for your reference:
For example, if a client maintains eligible funds of USD 18,000 during the settlement week, but only completes 2 standard lots of trading during this period. He/she will receive 0.05% daily interest for USD 10,000. The reward, calculated based on 0.05% x $10,000 x 5 days, yields a payout of USD 25 for this settlement week.
I didn't get any payout this week. Why might that be?
There are several reasons that may cause you not to receive the reward on the week:
You failed to meet the criteria for getting the reward from the previous settlement week
a. Your eligible funds fell below USD 5,000 on any day throughout the previous week.
b. You executed less than 1.0 standard lots per USD 5,000 in eligible funds during the previous week.
c. You made a withdrawal or internal fund transfer from participating accounts during the previous week.
The reward from the last settlement week will only be credited to you when the last settlement cycle ends.
I didn’t get the reward after the settlement cycle (every 4 weeks). Why might that be?
Your reward will be withheld if the following conditions are met when we try to credit the reward amount to you:
The account balance of your participation account is 0 on the settlement day, and
No trades opened on your participation account within the last 7 days from the settlement day, and
No active trading positions on your participation account on the settlement day.
Please contact us if you encounter any technical issues on this offer, our customer support team is here to assist you 24/7.
