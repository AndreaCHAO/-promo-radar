One licence, and it is not an Iranian one
TNFX Ltd holds one financial licence: SD133, Securities Dealer, from the Financial Services Authority of Seychelles. No Iranian authority has licensed, authorised or registered us — not the Central Bank of Iran, not the Securities and Exchange Organization — and we are not licensed by the FCA, ASIC or CySEC either.
There is also no Iranian retail forex or CFD licence for an offshore broker to hold, so a firm advertising Iranian authorisation is advertising something that does not exist.
What our licence costs you is specific: no Iranian supervision, no Iranian recourse, and a dispute heard in Seychelles. Check SD133 on the Seychelles register yourself rather than taking our word for it. Whether you may lawfully trade with an offshore broker, or move money to one, is a question for your own adviser and not advice we give.
Why a card or wire from an Iranian bank will not complete
Iranian banks sit outside the international payment networks. Shetab and Shaparak, the domestic switch and card systems, work inside Iran and nowhere else; Iranian banks do not issue Visa or Mastercard; and the large Iranian banks are disconnected from SWIFT.
At its June 2026 plenary the Financial Action Task Force again listed Iran as a high-risk jurisdiction subject to a call for action, and asked jurisdictions to apply countermeasures under its Recommendation 19 — among them limiting transactions and not establishing new correspondent banking relationships.
The practical result is that a deposit attempt from an Iranian-issued card or an Iranian bank is refused upstream, by the networks and the receiving bank, before it ever reaches our payment page. We cannot change that, and nothing on this page offers a way past it.
What funding an account from Iran actually looks like
The account is denominated in US dollars. There is no rial rail, no Iranian payment processor, and no funding route we can offer that starts at an Iranian bank.
In practice, funding an account from inside Iran rarely completes. Anything that depends on a bank is refused upstream, and the payment providers each set their own country rules and commonly decline a resident of Iran outright. That is their decision rather than ours, we promise none of them, and we will not suggest a way around a refusal.
Withdrawals are paid only to a method in the account holder's own name, never a third party, which binds the way out as tightly as the way in. Settle funding and payout before you open an account.
The half hour that breaks a news calendar
Iran runs three and a half hours ahead of Greenwich all year and abolished daylight saving in September 2022, so the offset no longer moves with the season. The platform clock runs three hours ahead of Greenwich, the same as Mecca time, which leaves a fixed thirty-minute gap: a candle stamped 15:00 on your chart opened at 15:30 in Tehran, and the daily candle rolls over at 00:30 local.
A half-hour offset is unusual, easy to dismiss, and exactly the size that ruins an economic calendar — you read a release as still half an hour away when the number has already printed. Work the difference out once, write it down, and keep it next to the calendar.
In local terms the London–New York overlap, the deepest liquidity of the day, covers your late afternoon and evening.
Swap-free accounts, the shape of your week, and the platform
A swap-free account is available on any account type on request, applied to your specific account rather than switched on by default. On one, no overnight financing is charged, none is earned, and no fee replaces it — but confirm it is active on your account before holding overnight. On a trade held for the interest-rate differential, cost and income vanish together, leaving direction as the only thing to be right about.
Your week and the market's do not line up. The Iranian working week runs Saturday to Wednesday, Thursday short or closed, Friday off — while the market opens in the small hours of Monday in Tehran and closes late Friday night. Two working days have no market, and US employment data on the first Friday lands on your day of rest.

Common questions
Is TNFX licensed or regulated in Iran? Opening the account is not the obstacle; funding it is, for the reasons set out above. Establish whether you can fund and be paid out before you spend any time on the account itself.
Can I deposit with an Iranian bank card or a wire from an Iranian bank? No, neither will complete. Can I hold my account in rial? No. Accounts are denominated in US dollars, and there is no rial rail and no Iranian payment processor, so no part of your balance is ever held in rial. Any currency conversion happens outside the account entirely, before the dollars reach it, and the dollar figure credited is what lands in your trading balance.

On a position you would otherwise hold for the carry, the cost and the income are both gone, which leaves direction as the only thing to be right about.
Does the platform clock match Tehran time? Not quite, and the gap is an unusual size. Iran is three and a half hours ahead of Greenwich year-round, with no daylight saving since September 2022, while the platform runs three hours ahead, the same as Mecca time.
