Gifts
At xChief, active traders not only benefit from their trading, they also gain access to exclusive gifts and rewards. These include stylish xChief-branded accessories and gadgets, as well as high-value items such as cars, motorcycles, electric scooters, MacBooks, iPhones, iPads, Apple Watches, AirPods, professional Samsung monitors, and more. The more actively you trade, the greater your chances of receiving these premium rewards.
Unfortunately, gifts are currently only available in the following countries: Indonesia , Iran , Nigeria .
We are working to make them available globally!
How to Receive Gifts
Register and open a trading account

Terms and Conditions
Accumulate points based on your trading turnover and redeem them for gifts! Points are calculated using the formula:
$500,000 turnover = 1 point
Example:
You closed 3 positions:
BUY 1 lot of EURUSD (1 lot = 100,000 EUR) order opened at a price of 1.02505 and closed at 1.03704. Trading turnover in USD for this order = (100,000 * 1.02505) + (100,000 * 1.03704) = 206,209 USD
SELL 1 lot of GOLD (ticker XAU/USD), where 1 lot = 100 oz. Order opened at a price of 1801.24 and closed at 1805.35. Trading turnover in USD for this order = (1801.24 * 100) + (1805.35 * 100) = 360,659 USD
SELL 1 lot of WTI Crude Oil (ticker XTI/USD), where 1 lot = 1000 barrels. Order opened at a price of 91.35 and closed at 94.50. Trading turnover in USD for this order = (91.35 * 1000) + (94.50 * 1000) = 185,850 USD
Total turnover = $206,209 + $360,659 + $185,850 = $752,718
You receive = $752,718 / $500,000 = 1.5 points
The amount of points is calculated based on the weekly trading turnover and credited once a week, at weekends.
Once the points are credited, please select and order Gifts from the Personal Area. Your personal manager will contact you to clarify the details of delivery.
Please note that turnover on accounts with an active No-Deposit Bonus, as well as on PAMM accounts, is not counted when calculating points.
