CMC Markets Signs Anthony Joshua as Ambassador Before His Fight With Tyson Fury
2026-11-17T10:48:05.183+02:00
T u e s d a y , 1 7 / 1 1 / 2 0 2 6 | 0 8 : 4 8 G M T by
Damian Chmiel
The 12-month deal puts the broker's logo on the boxer's shorts and walkout jacket in Cardiff on December 11.
His opponent fronts the largest ad campaign in rival XTB's history, launched in October.
Brokers
CMC Markets
Source: CMC Markets
CMC Markets has signed Anthony Joshua as a global ambassador for 12 months, the London-listed broker said today (Tuesday). The deal starts with its branding on the two-time heavyweight world champion's shorts and walkout jacket for his fight against Tyson Fury in Cardiff.
The December 11 bout will put two listed brokers in opposite corners. Polish broker XTB cast Fury alongside Zlatan Ibrahimović in what it called the largest advertising campaign in its history , announced in October.
Both sponsors head into it after a run on the stock market. CMC Markets' stock price gained 45.9% in the third quarter and XTB's 41.2% , FM Intelligence data show, while IG Group fell 27.1%.
London's trading industry is coming home!
Second Sports Deal Under One Banner
CMC tied the agreement to its For The Passion marketing platform, the same banner it used when it became Everton's front-of-shirt sponsor under a multi-year deal announced on July 1. Lord Peter Cruddas, its founder and chief executive, said the company would back its ambassador on fight night.
Lord Peter Cruddas, CMC Markets Chief Executive and Founder
"We wish both fighters the very best, but we'll be cheering for our AJ," Cruddas said.

To mark the launch, CMC Funded is giving away two pairs of VIP tickets. Dubai-based True North Tech runs that simulated prop trading program, which set October 1 as its public launch date and is not a regulated CMC Markets product, according to the release.
CMC said the partnership should help it bring new audiences to financial markets. Its home market draws the most broker-site traffic in Europe.
The UK recorded 13.9 million organic visits to retail broker websites in the third quarter, up 3% on the quarter, FM Intelligence's heat map shows .
Boxing Has Carried Broker Logos Before
Brokers have signed heavyweights before, and the shorts are a familiar spot. XTB sponsored Fury for his December 2024 rematch with Oleksandr Usyk , with its logo on his shorts and team outfits through 2025.
A month earlier, NAGA Group appointed Mike Tyson as its brand ambassador and filmed an advertisement with him for its trading app. Joshua's deal follows the XTB model on fight night, with branding on the same kit.
Joshua said the partnership "is about more than one night" and that he was looking forward to working with CMC over the next year.
After December 11, CMC Markets plans content, appearances and further fan events over the following 12 months, and said more details would come later.
