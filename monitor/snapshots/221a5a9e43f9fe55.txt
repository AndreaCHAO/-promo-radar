FAQ on this IB Support Program
How long is this IB Support Program valid for?
The program runs from 1 July 2026 to to 30 September 2026. All client deposits and trading volume must take place within this period to qualify.
When does the qualifying period start?
Your qualifying period starts from the date you opt in to the program, and runs until 30 September 2026. Note that if you opt out and re-join during the offer period, your qualifying period will restart from your latest opt-in date, and any performance prior to that date will not be carried forward.
What happens if I opt out and re-join during the offer period?
If you opt out and subsequently re-join the program before 30 September 2026, your qualifying period will restart from your latest opt-in date.
How do I win a prize under the IB Support Program?
To qualify for a prize, you need to meet both the respective net deposit (ND) and trading volume (NV) requirements for each tier from your opt-in date to
30 September 2026.
For purposes of this IB Support Program, only ND and NV from your directly referred clients will be taken into account. Take note that ND and NV from sub-IB(s) or downstream partner(s) will not be included.
Further, VT Markets reserves the right to cancel your reward if any suspicious activities are found, including but not limited to
a. Churning (opening and closing of Margin FX or CFD transactions just for the benefit of earning a benefit);
b. the partner’s client is found to violate its Client Agreement;
c. the partner is found to violate its Partner Agreement or has breached the terms and conditions of this offer.
How will I know if I have qualified for any prize(s)?
VT Markets will verify all ND and NV data as the campaign ends on 30 September 2026. Once verification is complete, you will be notified by email on the prize tier you have achieved. You do not need to submit a claim or check your status manually, as all eligible partners will be reached out directly.
How do I redeem my prize?
Upon receiving your qualification notification email, simply reply to confirm your redemption intent and indicate your preferred redemption option. All redemption confirmations must be received by 20 October 2026. Failure to respond by this deadline will result in automatic forfeiture of the prize.
Can the same client deposits and trading volume count towards both the IB Support program and other VT Markets promotions?
If any client deposits or trading volumes are used to claim other VT Markets promotions, the corresponding amount will be deducted from your total calculation for this offer. Duplicate claims across multiple programs using the same client data are prohibited. VT Markets reserves the right to disqualify ineligible claims or adjust reward allocations accordingly.
Can I choose a cash payment instead of a physical prize?
As every prize level has a cash equivalent option, you may elect to receive 80% of the prize value in USD instead of receiving the physical prize.
