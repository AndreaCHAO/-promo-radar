FAQs
How to join XS Partner Loyalty Program?
As an XS partner, you're automatically enrolled in the loyalty program. Your rewards are based on the number of active clients you've successfully brought in and the total volume of their trades.
How can I view my progress?
Log in XS Partner Portal, navigate to Partner Loyalty to view your progress towards meeting the requirements.
Is there a time limit to reach the next level?
There is no limit to how many prizes a partner can receive. As long as you fulfil the requirement, you are eligible to receive your prize.
Are there any limit to the number of prizes I can receive?
No. This program is designed to reward long-term partnership.
Can I opt for cash equivalent instead of the physical gift?
Yes. We understand that our global partners have different needs. While we are happy to deliver these luxury rewards, you have the flexibility to opt for cash equivalent listed in the XS Portal, which will be deposited directly into your XS wallet.
