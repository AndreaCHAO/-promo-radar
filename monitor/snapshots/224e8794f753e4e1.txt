FBS Workshop in Thailand Turns Beginners into Confident Traders
As part of a regular series of educational initiatives, FBS welcomed 80 traders and Partners to a hands-on workshop in Thailand on July 27. Partner Hirunyapong Wongfutan hosted the event, which was supported by FBS Manager Timarat Ngodsanthia.
The workshop brought together newcomers and more experienced traders eager to sharpen their skills or explore relevant ways to succeed in the market.
Here’s what was in store for the participants:
Introduction to FBS
Order Block Technical Trading for spotting high-probability market zones
Two practical strategies to win profits from the market
Using the Big Capitalist Tracking Tool to follow spot-on money moves
The sessions were led by speakers Mr. Hirunyapong Wongfutan, Mr. Boonyawat Suwan, and Mr. Phantawin Marayong.
Timarat Ngodsanthia was excited to see how active the participants were:
"What stood out to me was how quickly the new traders picked things up. Many had never heard of order block trading before, but by the end, they were discussing setups and putting forward their own ideas. I’m confident a lot of what they learned today will show up in their successful trades tomorrow."
The workshop was followed by a lively Q&A where participants could ask about their own trading challenges. The traditional FBS gift giveaway made the day even more memorable.
FBS’s mission is to make high-quality trading education accessible to everyone. This and future events help us build a strong, informed trading community where knowledge meets opportunity.
