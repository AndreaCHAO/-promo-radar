Terms and Conditions
The Welcome Bonus of up to $500 is available exclusively for the following trading account types: MT5.Classic+, MT5.DirectFX, and MT5.xPRIME .
The Welcome Bonus must be claimed during the initial deposit of at least $50 . To activate the bonus, select the "Enroll Welcome Bonus" option on the deposit form.
The Welcome Bonus is equal to 100% of the initial deposit , up to a maximum of $500 (or its equivalent in the trading account's base currency).
Trading profits may be withdrawn at any time, subject to the applicable withdrawal conditions. The Welcome Bonus becomes eligible for withdrawal only after completing the required trading turnover. The required trading turnover is calculated as follows:
Required Turnover = Welcome Bonus (USD) × 50,000 .
Example:
If a trader deposits $200 and receives a Welcome Bonus of $200, the required trading turnover will be $10,000,000 ($200 × 50,000), approximately equivalent to 44 standard lots of EURUSD, depending on execution prices.
Trading turnover is calculated as the total notional value of all opening and closing transactions executed on the trading account.
Examples:
BUY 1 lot of EURUSD (1 lot = 100,000 EUR) position opened at a price of 1.1257 and closed at 1.1283.
Trading turnover =
(100,000 * 1.1257) +
(100,000 * 1.1283) =
225,400 USD
SELL 5 lot of USDJPY (1 lot = 100,000 USD) position opened at a price of 109.806 and closed at 109.352.
Trading turnover =
(500,000 * 1) +
(500,000 * 1) =
1,000,000 USD
BUY 3.5 lot of GBPUSD (1 lot = 100,000 GBP) position opened at a price of 1.2978 and closed at 1.2985.
Trading turnover =
(350,000 * 1.2978) +
(350,000 * 1.2985) =
908,705 USD
The Welcome Bonus cannot be used to cover trading losses beyond the Client's own funds . If the account Equity falls to or below the amount of the active Welcome Bonus, the bonus will be automatically cancelled, and all open positions will be forcibly closed (Stop Out).
Example:
A trader deposits $500 and receives a Welcome Bonus of $500. If the account Equity falls to $500 (the amount reflected in the Credit field), the Welcome Bonus will be automatically cancelled, and all open positions will be forcibly closed (Stop Out).
While the Welcome Bonus remains active, the amount available for withdrawal is restricted and calculated as follows: Available for Withdrawal = Free Margin − Welcome Bonus − Qualifying Deposit Amount .
The Qualifying Deposit Amount refers to the initial deposit for which the Welcome Bonus was granted.
Clients may cancel the Welcome Bonus at any time through the Personal Area. Upon cancellation, the bonus amount will be deducted from the trading account, and the associated withdrawal restrictions will be removed.
To protect against potential abuse of the Negative Balance Protection Policy, the Company reserves the right to cancel the Welcome Bonus if the Client increases market exposure ahead of major economic news releases, market closures, or other events that may cause significant market volatility or price gaps.
