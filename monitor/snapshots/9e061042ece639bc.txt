FM-Events
FMLS 2026
CMC Markets founder Lord Peter Cruddas
Building a brokerage is one challenge; guiding it through decades of market, technology and regulatory change is another. CMC Markets founder Lord Peter Cruddas joins the Finance Magnates Summit London 2026 for a conversation about the decisions and turning points behind his career and the evolution of retail trading.
As CMC Markets’ founder, Lord Peter Cruddas has seen retail trading change across technology, distribution and customer expectations, he will share 40 years' worth of experience at the top, walking the Summit's attendees through some of the most decisive moments in his career and those still to come.
The story of CMC Markets is also a story about the evolution of retail trading in the United Kingdom. Lord Peter Cruddas founded the business and helped shape an industry that has transformed fundamentally, starting with the technological infrastructure and continuing with distribution and customer expectations.
At the Summit's 15th edition, in his interview with FInance Magnates, he will analyse brokerage transformation through the lens of leadership that has outlasted several market cycles, circling back to his personal experience at the helm of a multiregulated brokerage. .
The interview will explore the importance of conviction, the discipline required to keep innovating and the choices that turn an early-stage enterprise into an established financial services business. It is an opportunity to consider how founders balance long-term ambition with immediate commercial realities, particularly when markets and regulation change.
For brokers, fintech founders and executives, the relevance extends beyond company history. The questions of when to diversify, how to respond to new competitors and how to maintain an entrepreneurial culture are still central to the industry. This conversation connects the Summit's anniversary with the strategic choices facing businesses today.
Join the session at 11:00 - 11:30 on 24 November and explore the full agenda at https://events.financemagnates.com/events/fmsummitlondon/agenda/schedule
This personal perspective matters because the pressures facing brokers today are not entirely new. Shifts in technology, distribution and client expectations have repeatedly forced firms to reconsider their business models. Understanding how established leaders responded can inform the next set of decisions.
The interview is part of a wider day focused on commercial intelligence. Attendees can follow the interview in parallel with discussions on brokerage growth, AI and market infrastructure and also meet participating firms at the exhibition to explore how those themes translate into practical business choices.
