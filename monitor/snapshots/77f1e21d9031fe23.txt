Conditions
1- Only verified clients can participate in the contest.
2- Each client can participate with one account only.
3- Participation is limited to one time (no restarts and no additional funds).
4- Double trading between participants and the use of multiple email addresses by a single client are prohibited.
5- Client must achieve a minimum trading volume of 5 lots and 10 trades using the participated account.
6- All positions will be automatically closed at the end of the contest.
7- The final equity must exceed the starting balance upon conclusion of the contest.
8- Both manual and EA trading are allowed for this contest.
Ranking Calculation
The leaderboard will be updated daily at the frequency of 20 minutes during the contest period based on the formula below.
(Equity/Initial Balance) x 100
Example:
Starting balance = $10,000
Equity = $11,000
Calculation:
($11,000 ÷ $10,000) x 100% = 110%
Winners Announcement
1- At the end of the contest, all positions will be closed and accounts set to read-only.
2- XS will review the leaderboard and disqualify any non-eligible participants.
3- Disqualified accounts’ equity will be set to zero.
4- Top 10 winners who qualified will be the winner for the contest. In the event of a performance tie between clients, the client with more trading lots and more positions will be ranked higher.
5- Winners will be contacted by email and announced on our official website within five (5) business days after the contest ends.
6- All prizes will be deposited directly into the winners’ XS wallets.
