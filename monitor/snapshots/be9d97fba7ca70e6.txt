WeTrade at Money Expo Colombia 2026
WeTrade
WeTrade will be participating in Money Expo Colombia 2026, on June 24-25th, at the Ágora Bogotá Centro de Convenciones. This event stands as one of the most relevant gatherings in the financial sector across the region, bringing together industry leaders, traders, and investors.
As a brand built on trust, transparency, and performance, WeTrade sees Money Expo Colombia as more than just an expo, it is a meeting point where conversations go beyond products and focus on shared challenges, real opportunities, and the future of the financial ecosystem in Latin America.
What to Expect at our Booth
When you visit us at Booth #17, we invite you to connect directly with the WeTrade team and discover firsthand how our platform supports your trading experience. Whether you want to explore our trading solutions, learn about partnership opportunities as an Introducing Broker, or simply exchange market perspectives, our team will be on hand to share knowledge and insights with you.
Don't Miss Our Conferences
Beyond visiting our booth, you will have the opportunity to attend our conferences, where WeTrade experts will cover key topics shaping today's market, from trading strategies to the trends transforming the financial landscape across the region. It is a unique opportunity to learn, ask questions, and walk away with actionable ideas.
We look forward to reconnecting with familiar faces and welcoming new ones. Come experience our brand up close and see how trust continues to drive everything we do.
See you in Bogotá!
