Frequently Asked Questions
What is a CPA Affiliate?
A CPA affiliate (Cost Per Acquisition) is someone who earns a one-time commission for referring qualified clients to TMGM. By sharing unique referral links and employing strategic marketing, you can convert your online traffic into a steady income. As part of TMGM’s forex affiliate program , you promote our trading services and get paid when someone you refer signs up and meets the criteria. It’s a powerful way to monetize your audience using CPA affiliate marketing .
How do I become a CPA Affiliate?
Getting started as a CPA affiliate is easy:
1. Sign up through the TMGM Partners website and join the forex CPA affiliate program
2. Get your unique referral link and marketing assets
3. Promote TMGM to your audience using banners, landing pages, or social media
4. Earn commissions when your referred users become qualified clients through CPA affiliate marketing

What are the requirements needed to start earning commissions as a CPA Affiliate?
To earn as a CPA affiliate , your referrals must meet TMGM’s qualification criteria, which typically include verifying their account and making a first-time deposit. Once that happens, your commission is automatically credited through the forex affiliate program .
What are the commission rates for a CPA Affiliate?
As a CPA affiliate , you can earn up to $600 per qualified client referral. The exact amount depends on the client’s region, traffic source, and the quality of the referral.
