How the money is actually calculated
Your commission is a share of the trading volume of the clients under you, credited per lot, and it does not depend on whether that client won or lost. It is paid on activity, which is the only model that keeps a partner's interest and a client's interest pointing the same way.
Two mechanics are worth understanding before you start, because they are the two questions every new partner eventually asks:
Sub-IB levels. Partners you introduce become partners in their own right, and you earn a smaller share of their network's volume as well as your own clients'.
The short-trade rule. Positions opened and closed inside five minutes do not count toward commission. This exists so that nobody can generate volume by opening and closing instantly, and it is worth telling your clients about rather than letting them discover it in a statement.
Reporting is live. Volume, clients and earnings update as trades close — you are not waiting for a monthly statement to find out where you stand.

The rules that protect both of us
A partner introduces clients. That is the whole job, and the boundaries around it are there because breaking them is what gets partners and brokers into trouble:
Never promise a return. Not a monthly percentage, not a guaranteed profit. Real trading has no fixed return, and promising one is the signature of every scheme that has collapsed in this region.
Never trade on a client's behalf or ask for their password. If they want someone else's decisions, that is what copy trading is for.
No self-referral. Introducing yourself under another name, or routing your own trading through your own IB code, is not commission — it is a rebate on your own activity, and it is reversed when found.
Be accurate about costs. Spreads, swaps and the deposit routes that actually pay out. A client who feels misled leaves, and you lose the volume you were paid on.
Common questions
How much does an IB earn? Commission is paid per traded lot and depends on your tier and client volume. The partner portal shows your exact rate and earnings.
When am I paid? Commission accrues as your clients trade and is paid out through the partner portal to local methods.
Do I need a licence to be an IB? You introduce clients; you do not handle their money or trade for them. Do not manage anyone's account or take deposits yourself.
Can I see my clients' activity? You see volume and commission data — not their personal trading positions or private details.
Do very short trades count toward my commission? Positions opened and closed within five minutes do not count. The rule exists to stop volume being generated artificially.
How do I know a client is tracked to me? They have to arrive through your referral link. Sharing the plain website address does not attach them to you, and it cannot be corrected reliably afterwards.
Can I refer myself, or my own family? You cannot refer yourself — routing your own trading through your own code is reversed when found. Family members trading their own money on their own accounts are ordinary clients.
