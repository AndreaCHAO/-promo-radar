What is an Introducing Broker (IB)?
An Introducing Broker (IB) serves as a bridge between traders and brokers, facilitating connections by introducing potential traders to brokers. The IB earns commissions or rebates based on the trades executed by the traders they introduce to the broker. By partnering with an IB, traders can gain access to brokerage services and potentially take advantage of the perks and resources offered by the broker.

What does an Introducing Broker (IB) do?
An Introducing Broker (IB) plays a crucial role in connecting traders with brokers. They act as intermediaries, facilitating the introduction between traders and brokers. Additionally, IBs often provide valuable support and guidance to their clients by offering consultation, answering queries, and even providing educational resources to enhance traders' skills.
To be effective, IBs need to possess a deep understanding of financial markets and stay updated with the latest news and trends. By staying informed, IBs can offer their clients the best possible service and advice, ensuring their long-term success. They aim to cultivate enduring relationships with their clients, guiding them through the intricacies of the financial markets and promoting their trading achievements.

When and how are my IB Rebates paid?
IB rebates are paid the day after one of your referred clients closes a trade.
For example, if the client closes their trade on Monday, the IB rebates would be paid on Tuesday.
Once your IB rebates have been transferred to your trading account, you can make a regular withdrawal from your client portal.
Can I see the clients who have signed up under my IB referral link?
