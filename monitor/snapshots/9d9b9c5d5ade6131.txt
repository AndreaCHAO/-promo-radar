Frequently asked questions
What is the Exness Partner Loyalty Program?
As an Exness partner, you're automatically enrolled in the loyalty program. Here you can earn exclusive prizes based on your number of active referred clients and their trading volume.
How can I receive prizes in the Loyalty Program?
You must meet the active referred client and trading volume requirements in order to qualify for a prize. Higher trading volumes unlock bigger prizes.
What prizes can I receive?
The loyalty program offers a variety of prizes, including high-end electronics, luxury items, travel experiences, and cash alternatives.
How do I check my progress and receive my prizes?
You can track your progress on your partner dashboard. Once you're eligible for a prize, you'll receive a qualification email, and your account manager will contact you within two weeks.
Is there a limit to the number of prizes I can receive?
There is no limit. As long as you fulfill the requirements, you are eligible to receive your prize.
Where can I find more information?
Please visit our Help Center to learn more about the program and its benefits.
