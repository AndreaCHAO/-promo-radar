EBC Autumn Trading Festival 2026 Offers USD$7,400 in Prizes
Summary:
EBC's 45-day Autumn Trading Festival offers US$7,400 across 10 prize positions, ranking qualifying PRO and STD accounts by percentage return until 16 October.
EBC Financial Group (EBC) is running its Autumn Trading Festival 2026 with USD$7,400 in prizes. The 45-day live competition runs from 1 September to 16 October 2026 in Japan Standard Time (JST), while registration remains open until 2 October.
The competition is open to qualifying PRO and STD accounts, with participants ranked by percentage return, so accounts of different sizes compete on equal footing.
Percentage Return Decides the Leaderboard
Each account is measured by percentage return, putting smaller and larger accounts on the same playing field. The calculation begins with the account's equity at entry and adjusts for deposits and withdrawals made during the competition.
Additional deposits increase the capital used to calculate the return rate. Withdrawals are added back to the trading result, so withdrawing funds does not reduce the return used for ranking.
Existing and New Accounts Can Enter
There is no restriction based on when an account was created or when the user last traded. Existing and newly created PRO and STD accounts can enter when they meet the following conditions:
Account equity of at least USD300.
No open positions at the time of entry.
The account is not following another trading signal.
A maximum of 10 participating accounts per user.
Participants must publish a signal in EBC's copy trading community and keep the signal and required trading information public for the remainder of the competition.
Prizes Extend to 10th Place
The top prize is USD$3,000, with prizes available through 10th place.

EBC's Institutional-Grade Trading Environment
EBC's trading infrastructure connects to more than 25 top-tier liquidity venues and uses smart order routing to process orders. EBC reports that its platform processes more than 1,000 orders per second, with an average execution speed of around 20 milliseconds and a data-transmission stability rate of 98.75%. EBC was also named Best Broker for Execution at the Professional Trader Awards 2025 .
These figures are based on historical and simulated system performance under specific market conditions. They do not guarantee future execution speeds or fill rates, which can vary with market volatility, liquidity, and system latency.
Enter the Competition in Three Steps
Log in to the EBC Client Portal and open the event page.
Select Join Now and choose a qualifying PRO or STD account.
Agree to publish the required copy trading signal and complete the registration.
After registration, the participating account will appear in the My Ranking section of the event page.
Registration closes on 2 October 2026. Participants should review the complete competition rules before entering.
