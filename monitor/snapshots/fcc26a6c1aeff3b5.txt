EBC Indonesia Promotes Risk Literacy Through "Merdeka Trader Cup 2026" Demo Trading Competition
Summary:
EBC launches an Indonesia Independence Day demo trading challenge with USD 10,000 virtual equity and a risk-free institutional-grade experience.
The spirit of the Republic of Indonesia's Independence Day in August often serves as a momentum for the public to reflect on freedom, including the pursuit of "financial independence." Unfortunately, the growing public interest in investing and trading is frequently not matched by adequate risk management literacy, leaving many beginners vulnerable to avoidable losses due to a lack of preparation.
Addressing this challenge, EBC Indonesia is taking a proactive step by hosting the "Merdeka Trader Cup 2026." Unlike conventional competitions, this event focuses on nationwide education, challenging participants to test global market trading strategies 100% risk-free using an MT4 Demo account.
"Financial independence cannot be achieved through mere recklessness. It requires experience, discipline, and a mature understanding of risk. Through the Merdeka Trader Cup 2026, we aim to provide a safe learning environment. Participants can experience genuine global market dynamics without worrying about losing a single penny," explained Fikri Fairuz, Marketing Strategist at EBC Indonesia.
Real Simulation to Train Psychology and Strategy
To provide a comprehensive learning experience, EBC Indonesia equips every verified participant with a demo account loaded with a virtual balance of USD 10,000 and leverage of 1:500. This facility is designed so that participants, both Beginner Traders and Existing Traders can simulate their strategies across various instruments, including Major Forex Pairs, Gold (XAU/USD), Crude Oil, as well as US, European, and Asian Stock Indices.
Education Through Scoring: Profit vs. Risk
The educational value of this competition is heavily emphasized in its judging criteria. Winners will not be evaluated solely on the percentage of profit or Return on Investment (ROI) generated.
To emphasize the importance of capital preservation, EBC Indonesia has introduced an additional evaluation metric. In the event of a tie in ROI scores, the winner will be determined by the lowest Maximum Drawdown. This teaches that successful traders are not those who blindly chase high profits, but those who disciplinedly manage potential losses.
As an appreciation for top discipline and best strategy, EBC Indonesia has prepared a total prize pool worth hundreds of dollars for the 10 most consistent performers, with the following breakdown:
1st Place: USD300
2nd Place: USD200
3rd Place: USD100
4th–5th Place: USD50 each
6th–10th Place: USD30 each
Low-Risk, No-Risk to Experience EBC's Institutional-Grade Trading Environment
The Merdeka Trader Cup 2026 provides participants with a low-risk, no-risk setting to test their strategies because every trade is placed with virtual funds rather than live capital. Traders can try different approaches, observe how they perform as markets move, and evaluate the results without losing real money. It also gives participants a practical introduction to the professional trading environment before they decide whether they are ready to trade with live capital.
Behind EBC's trading platform is institutional-grade infrastructure built to handle orders quickly and connect traders to deep liquidity. EBC connects directly to more than 25 top-tier liquidity venues through its professional HUB infrastructure and Smart Order Routing engine. The company's published platform figures include more than 1,000 orders processed per second, an average execution speed of 20 milliseconds, and 98.75% data-transmission stability.
Building a Healthy Trading Ecosystem
Registration for the Merdeka Trader Cup 2026 is open for free from August 3 to August 31, 2026.
Let's make this August the first step toward true financial independence. Register now at https://www.ebcidn.com/trading-cup-demo2/id and hone your skills at the Merdeka Trader Cup 2026!
