From trader to partner: Turn your network into income
If you trade regularly in the financial markets, sharing your experience often comes naturally. You might post market analysis, share setup ideas, or host a trading group for like-minded traders.
But what if that same activity could become a way to earn income?
By registering for our Exness Partnership Program , you can refer other traders to Exness and receive commission from their trading activity—earning double what you would with other brokers. 1
Keep reading to discover why traders choose Exness as their partner, and what makes earning twice as much possible.
How can traders become Exness partners?
If you’re already sharing what you know about trading, you can build on that experience by partnering with Exness as an Introducing Broker (IB) .
As an IB, you refer traders in your network to Exness and earn commission from their trading activity.
And you wouldn’t be the first trader to make that move. 95.2% of Exness IBs started as traders , 2 with active IBs earning an average monthly commission of over 3,600 USD in 2025. 3
Once established, your earnings depend on your referrals’ trading activity and how our revenue share model rewards it.

How does revenue share turn referrals into earnings?
With our revenue share model, you earn commission when your referred clients trade with Exness. Your earnings are linked to their ongoing trading activity, not a once-off referral.
Here’s what that means for you:
Earn up to 40%: Depending on your partner level, you can earn up to 40% of the spread from your referred clients’ trades.
Create a more consistent income stream: When your referred clients keep trading, their eligible trades continue to earn you commission.
Build on the network you already have: The model can suit educators, mentors, and people who run trading communities or networks.
Get paid your way: Your commission will be instantly credited 4 to your partner account.
Get your commission whenever you need: You can withdraw your commission instantly. 5
But there’s another reason partners can earn more with Exness: more of their referrals’ trades count toward commission.
Why Exness partners earn 2x higher revenue
Exness partners earn double compared to other brokers because we remove common barriers.
As an IB, you can earn commission on 100% of your referred clients’ eligible trades, 6 including short trades. In fact, more than 56% of Exness partner rewards have come from trades held for under three minutes. 7
As a 3-minute trade duration is a common payout threshold among brokers, models that apply this restriction exclude more than half of the potential commission.
Exness partners also enjoy lifetime payouts. It’s not only new referrals that count. Existing referrals can keep earning you commission for as long as they are actively trading.
When more of your referrals’ trades qualify for commission, more of their trading activity can contribute to your earnings.
What can partners earn beyond commission?
Partner commission is the primary way you earn, but it’s not the only reward available.
Through our Partner Loyalty Program, eligible partners can earn up to 500,000 USD in extra rewards . 8 As they reach program milestones, they move up through the tiers and can earn bigger rewards, including luxury items and travel opportunities.
Partners can also unlock access to exclusive events, learning opportunities, and support from dedicated account managers.
Is becoming an Exness partner your next step?
If you already share trading ideas, market analysis, or educational content with other traders, you may already have the starting point for a partner network.
Becoming an Exness IB lets you build on that network and earn from the trading activity of the clients you refer.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need a minimum number of referrals to start earning?
No. You start earning commission from your first active referred client. 9 You don’t need a minimum number of active referrals to start earning.
Do I need to trade with Exness to become an Introducing Broker (IB)?
No. You don’t need to be trading with Exness to become an Exness IB. Exness partners can include traders, educators, mentors, content creators, and people who run trading communities.
Is there a limit to how much partner commission I can earn?
There’s no fixed cap on partner commission. How much you earn depends on factors like your partner level and the eligible trading activity of your referred clients.
How fast can I get my partner commission?
You can withdraw your commission instantly. 5 99% of partner withdrawals are processed automatically, 10 with an average processing time of 2.2 minutes. 11
