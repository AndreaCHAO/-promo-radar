When calculating lot size, please consider the following: If a trading asset's one point cost differs from the EURUSD's one point cost, the volumes traded in such assets will be converted to EURUSD volumes. For example, one point cost per 1 lot in EURUSD is $1. One point cost per 1 lot in EURCHF is $1.2151. As a result, the EURCHF trading volume will be multiplied by 1.2151. It means 1 lot traded in EURCHF will be considered as 1.2151 lots.
