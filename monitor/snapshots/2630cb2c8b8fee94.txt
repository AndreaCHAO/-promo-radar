What’s the difference between the Pepperstone Affiliate and IB programs?
The key distinction between the two programs is the payment structure. Affiliates typically receive a cost per acquisition (CPA), meaning a one-time payment is received for each new client introduced, whereas IBs receive ongoing payment as the client’s trade volume increases. 1
Am I eligible to become an Affiliate?
Here are the eligibility criteria:
Having an active online channel that provides impartial finance or trading-related information to prospective customers;
You are a legal resident of or incorporated in any of the allowed countries;
The vast majority of traffic is generated from the allowed countries;
Your traffic source meets the compliance standards: there is no promise of unreasonable trading profit;
there is no direct trading advice unless you are officially licenced to do so;
a correct Risk Warning is present and not concealed by their size, colour, or in any other way;
the content is balanced and not misleading;
there are no visuals that may imply unrealistic gains;
promoted trading conditions are relevant to the Pepperstone target audience (e.g. no binary options; funded accounts);
The services provided by you are allowed in Pepperstone;
Your traffic source does not disguise itself as the official channel of Pepperstone.

What is my CPA or commission?
As a client reaches specific goals on their journey, you would receive the allocated commission within the commission structure, according to the country the said client registered from. The commission amount varies from USD 300 to USD 800 per referral depending on their designated country group. At Pepperstone, we always try to offer commission-increase opportunities for top performers and we are open to custom deals.
