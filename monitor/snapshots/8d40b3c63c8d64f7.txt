Tickmill Introducing Brokers compete to win a share of $45,000: Tickmill launches IB Trading Cup 2026
Tickmill is pleased to announce the launch of the IB Trading Cup 2026, a global Introducing Broker competition designed to reward performance, engagement, and client growth across its international IB network. Kicking off the same time as the FIFA World Cup and running from 11 June 2026 to 14 August 2026, the competition brings together IBs from around the world to compete across five regional groups for a share of a $45,000 prize pool.
Participants will earn points based on their underlying clients, with rankings updated throughout the competition period. The top-performing IBs in each group will be rewarded for their ability to grow client engagement in a highly competitive environment.
Entry into the IB Trading Cup 2026 is open to all approved Tickmill Introducing Brokers. Existing IBs are automatically enrolled, while new participants can join by opening an IB account via the Client Area. IBs earn contest points based on trading volume generated by their referred clients, which contributes to the IB’s score. Only new clients registered during the competition period are eligible, and performance is measured exclusively on closed trades.
Each IB is assigned to one of five regional groups and competes within their designated leaderboard, where the top five performers from each group will receive cash prizes ranging from $500 to $5,000.
Tickmill Group Chief Commercial Officer Nicholas Baumer commented: “The IB Trading Cup 2026 reflects our ongoing commitment to empowering our global Introducing Broker network. This competition is designed to reward performance, dedication, and client growth on a truly international scale. Our IBs are a vital part of the Tickmill ecosystem, and we are proud to provide them with an engaging, competitive environment where their efforts directly translate into meaningful rewards. We invite all eligible partners to take part, build momentum, and compete for top positions in their respective groups.”
About Tickmill
Tickmill is a globally recognised, award-winning, multi-regulated broker providing access to forex and CFD trading across a wide range of asset classes. Built on a foundation of transparency, advanced technology, and competitive trading conditions, Tickmill serves clients in over 180 countries and supports a rapidly expanding network of Introducing Brokers worldwide.
With more than 11 years of industry experience, the company has facilitated over 830 million trades, achieved an average monthly trading volume of approximately $196 billion, and welcomed over 321,000 new traders since 2025.
