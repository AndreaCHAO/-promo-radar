FAQs
What is the XS Trading Miles loyalty program?
The XS Trading Miles program is a loyalty initiative designed to reward clients for their trading activity. You earn XS Miles with every eligible trade on XS.com, which can later be redeemed for cash rewards.
Tips: You'll earn more Miles when you're upgraded to a higher loyalty tier with XS.
*Selected products and account types only.Terms and Conditions apply.
How do I start earning XS Miles?
Getting started is simple. Just trade on selected products using an eligible account type.
For each lot traded on eligible account types and instruments held for over 2 minutes, the Miles will be automatically accumulated based on your current loyalty tier.
If you're a new client, register here and then start trading to earn your XS Miles.
How are loyalty tiers determined, and what are the different tiers?
Your loyalty tier is based on on your trading volume (NV) from closed trades in the past 90 days. The tiers range from "Trader" to "Top Trader," with specific notional volume requirements designed to reward your trading activity.
How are loyalty tiers reviewed and updated?
Promotions: Reviewed daily, so you could be upgraded to a higher tier as early as the next day if you meet the trading volume requirement.
Demotions: Evaluated on the first day of each month, or if there's inactivity for 20 consecutive days.
Which trading instruments and account types are eligible for earning XS Miles?
You can earn XS Miles by trading on Standard, Classic, Extra, Plus, Pro, and Elite accounts. Eligible instruments include Forex, Metals, Oil, and BTC. To accumulate XS Miles, positions must be held for at least 2 minutes.
Are there any maximum limits on the amount of XS Miles I can earn per month?
No, there's no maximum limit! You can earn as many XS Miles as your trading activity allows.
How do I redeem my XS Miles?
To redeem your XS Miles,
1) Log in to your XS Client Portal.
2) Navigate to the "Redeem XS Miles" page.
3) Select the reward you wish to redeem and submit. Once approved, cash will be credited to your wallet.
Where can I check my current XS Miles balance and loyalty tier?
You can access your XS Miles balance and Loyalty Tier through the client portal under XS Miles section
How long does it take to receive the cash after redemption?
Once your redemption form is submitted, XS will review it within 5 working days.
Cash reward: After approval, the reward will be credited to your wallet automatically.
