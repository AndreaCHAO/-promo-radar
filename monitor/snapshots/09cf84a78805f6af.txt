Commission structure for IB: Revenue share
Exness uses a revenue share model for IBs, allowing you to earn a percentage of the revenue generated from trades made by your referred clients.
This model provides ongoing earnings linked directly to your referred clients' trading activity.
What is Revenue share?
Exness generates revenue from the spread, the difference between bid and ask prices.
As an IB, you can earn up to 40% of the spread cost paid by your referred clients.
For instance, if Exness profits 100 USD from a referred client's transactions, you could earn 40 USD.

Frequently asked questions
What types of commissions does Exness offer to partners?
Exness provides a flexible commission structure, including a revenue share for IBs and a CPA model for affiliates. Partners can select the option that aligns with their business objectives.
How much can I earn with Exness partnership programs?
Introducing brokers (IBs) can enjoy up to a 40% revenue share and under the Cost Per Action (CPA) model, affiliates can earn up to 1,750 USD per referred client. CPA earnings depend on the referred client's first-time deposit (FTD), country of residence, and trading activity, providing a substantial income opportunity. Revenue share is calculated based on referred clients' trading volume.
How are commissions paid out?
Commissions are paid out daily, ensuring affiliates and IBs receive their earnings promptly. Exness offers seamless withdrawals for commission.
How does the revenue share model work for IBs?
In the revenue share model, IBs earn a percentage of profits derived from the spreads on trades made by their referred clients. This provides ongoing income tied directly to trading activity.
Are commissions calculated differently based on account type?
Yes, the commission calculation is based on the type of trading account used by your referred client.
What's the maximum amount I can earn?
The commission you can earn is 20% to 40%, depending on the number of your referred clients and their trading volume.
