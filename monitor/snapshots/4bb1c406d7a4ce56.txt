Calculation examples
Calculation of cashback from AMarkets
Example 1
Instruments: EUR/USD, USD/JPY
Trading volume in EUR/USD per trading session: 3 lots
Trading volume in USD/JPY per trading session: 0.9 lot
Current cashback level: 3 (cashback rate $13 per lot)
Holding period for each transaction is more than 3 minutes
The difference between the opening and closing prices of each transaction was at least 10 pips.
EUR/USD: open price 1.06925, close price 1.07052 (12.7 pips difference),
USD/JPY: open price 131.632, close price 131.52 (11.2 pips difference).
Cashback amount: 3 lots * $13 + 0.9 lot * $13 = $50.7
Example 2
Instruments: GBP/USD, NZD/JPY, XAU/USD (gold)
Trading volume in GBP/USD per trading session: 4.2 lots
Trading volume in NZD/JPY per trading session: 0.05 lot
Trading volume in XAU/USD per trading session: 0.2 lot
Current cashback level: 5 (cashback rate $17 per lot)
Holding period for each transaction is more than 3 minutes
The difference between the opening and closing prices of each transaction:
GBP/USD:
- for 4 lots: open price 1.25297, close price 1.2519 (10.7 pips difference),
- for 0.2 lot: open price 1.25297, close price 1.2520 (9.7 pips difference) -
doesn’t meet the cashback terms as it’s less than 10 pips.
NZD/JPY: open price 85.495, close price 85.70 (20.5 pips difference),
XAU/USD: open price $1842.26, close price $1842.50 (24 pips difference).
Cashback Amount: 4 lots * $17 + 0.05 lot * $17 + 0.2 lot * $17 = $72.25
