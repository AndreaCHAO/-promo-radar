Programme Guidelines
1. Follower Definitions
Follower Capital = Account Balance ÷ Number of followed Provider
Follower Limit: Each account can follow up to 10 providers simultaneously.
Valid Follower Account: An account is considered valid if its follower capital is at least $500. Accounts with less than $500 will not be counted as valid follower accounts and will not be included in the total follower capital.
Valid Follower User: If multiple valid accounts under the same EBC user follow the same trader (provider), they are counted as one valid follower user.
AUM(Assets Under Management):Total Follower Capital + Trader’s (Provider Account) Balance
2. Level Activation & Reward Criteria
Level Activation:
The system evaluates every hour. If both AUM and Valid Follower User Count meet the requirements of a particular level, that level is considered activated.
Reward Criteria (all conditions must be met):
The trader must maintain the required level on every calendar day of the month.
Effective Trading Volume (lot)≥ Trader’s (Provider Account) Daily Average Balance × 0.2%:
Effective Trading Volume = Total Closed Volume for the Month + Net Open Positions (Net Open Positions = Total Open Volume − Hedging Positions in the Same Symbol)
Daily Average Balance = Sum of Trader’s Daily Balances ÷ Number of Calendar Days in the Month
US stocks and ETFs are excluded from the effective trading volume calculation.
Bonus Calculation & Distribution
Daily Average AUM = Sum of Daily AUM ÷ Number of Calendar Days in the Month
Monthly Bonus = (Daily Average AUM × Management Fee Rate) ÷ 12
All rewards are credited to the trading account and can be withdrawn freely.
Participants whose identity information or trading activities are identified by the system or through manual verification as abnormal will have their eligibility and rewards cancelled.
This event is open only to valid clients. EBC reserves the right of final interpretation of the event rules and the right to amend the terms without prior notice.
