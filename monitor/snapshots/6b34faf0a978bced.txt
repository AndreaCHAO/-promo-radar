FAQs
What is Vantage Partner Program?
Vantage Partner Program is the partnership program of award winning multi-asset broker, Vantage. Partner with us as a CPA affiliate or Introducing Broker for some of the most competitive and flexible programs in the industry.
How do I partner with a Forex Broker?
To be a partner with a Forex broker, you can sign up with a broker like Vantage that provides a partnership program. At Vantage, you can effortlessly join as a Forex partner in just six simple steps.
Learn more about the process and how to join us by visiting our website or the article here .
Are there any fees to join the Vantage Forex Partner Program?
No, there are no fees to join the Vantage Forex Partner Program. Signing up to become an Affiliate or IB is fast, easy and absolutely free of charge.
How much can I earn with the Forex Partner Program?
The earning potential in our Forex Partner Program varies depending on the specific program you join:
● CPA Affiliate Program: If you are an affiliate acquiring leads and new customers from tier 1 countries, you can earn substantial rewards . Specifically, you can receive up to $1200 CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) payout for each client that you successfully refer .
● Introducing Broker (IB) Program: As an Introducing Broker, you can earn up to $8 in rebates for each trade that your referred clients execute. This program allows you to scale up your business by leveraging your network to increase trading volume.
Both programs are designed to provide earning opportunities based on your ability to attract and maintain active clients.
Which Forex partner program is more suitable for me?
It depends on the method you'll use to refer clients. If you provide your clients with any services, then the IB program would be better for you. On the other hand, the Forex CPA program is suitable for digital marketers who rely mostly on click tracking to source their referrals. Ideally, there would be no services or relationship shared with your leads.
Can I switch to another Vantage partner program after submitting the application?
Yes, you are advised to discuss with your Account Manager about switching your partner program before applying for it.
How can I attract leads and clients?
The promotional methods you use are entirely up to you and should fit in with your overall Affiliate or Introducing Broker business. We provide a selection of affiliate marketing tools and guidelines on our Vantage Partners website to get you started.
