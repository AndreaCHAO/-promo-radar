CPA commission structure
The finance affiliate niche is highly lucrative.
The Exness CPA model offers scalable earning potential.
Affiliates receive performance-based CPA commissions per referred client, depending on their location and first-time deposit amount.
What is the CPA model?
CPA stands for Cost Per Action.
It's an advertising model that rewards you when a unique new client (referral) completes a specific action.
For our program, your clients are required to complete their first-time deposit (FTD) and show trading activity.
