Introducing Brokers program
Introducing Broker (IB) program is a partnership program that allows you to receive commissions in return for Introducing clients to XS.
The IB program at XS enables you to work from anywhere, and even promote through your website and/or social media pages. It is designed to provide a wide range of benefits, valuable resources and outstanding support.
The program includes a dedicated relationship manager to help you grow your business and ensure that the clients get the best user experience and support.
In addition, you will have access to advanced tracking tools, exclusive marketing materials and promotional content, and you will be able to withdraw your earnings through multiple flexible and reliable payout options.
Why become an introducing broker with XS?
Generous rewards:
Earn the highest payouts by referring clients to a global market leader.
Prompt payouts:
Your payouts are paid daily, & you can access them whenever you want.
Advanced reporting:
Our advanced reporting tools allow you to monitor real-time statistics.
Promotional tools:
We provide you with the online tools you need to promote our products.
Marketing resources:
We support all of your offline marketing activities including events.
Excellent support:
Benefit from our high-quality relationship management and support.
Premium conditions:
The best conditions for you & the best experience for your clients.
